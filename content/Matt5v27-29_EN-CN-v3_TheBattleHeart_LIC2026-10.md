#### Introduction / 引言

In late 2007, an unusual legal report from the provincial court of Khon Kaen in northeastern Thailand made national headlines.
2007年底，来自泰国东北部孔敬府法院的一份不同寻常的法律报告登上了全国头条。


Over a single 90-day window—between July 1st and September 30th—the family court recorded a sudden wave of 142 divorce petitions.
在短短90天的时间里——从7月1日到9月30日——家事法庭记录了142起突如其来的离婚申请潮。

But what caught the attention of court reporters and sociologists wasn't just the sheer number; it was the exact demographic pattern.
但引起法庭记者和社会学家注意的不仅是这庞大的数字，更是其确切的人口特征模式。

Every single one of those 142 divorces was filed by a Thai woman seeking to dissolve her marriage to a Western expatriate husband.
这142起离婚诉讼无一例外都是由泰国女性提出的，要求解除与西方外籍丈夫的婚姻。

When interviewed by local news outlets, the Chief Justice of the Family Court summarized the core issue in a single word: **disillusionment**.
在接受当地新闻媒体采访时，家事法庭的首席法官用一个词总结了核心问题：**幻灭**。

142 women had entered into cross-cultural unions with high expectations—perhaps seeking economic security, emotional stability, or romantic fulfillment—only to find, within months or a few short years, that the promise had evaporated into thin air.[^1]
142名女性带着极高的期望进入跨文化婚姻——也许是寻找经济保障、情感稳定或浪漫满足——却在几个月或短短几年内发现，当初的许诺已化为乌有。

What happened in Khon Kaen in 2007 is likely an expected result of entering a relationship or marriage with unrealistic romantic and selfish expectations, while asking, “What’s in it for me?”
2007年在孔敬发生的事情，很可能是带着不切实际的浪漫与自私期望进入关系或婚姻、同时不断问“我能从中得到什么？”的必然结果。

We also live in a very sexually permissive world, and country:
我们今天也生活在一个性观念非常开放的世界和国家：

A 2025 survey ranks Thailand as the country with the highest global infidelity rate, with 51% of respondents admitting to extramarital affairs.
2025年的一项调查将泰国列为全球外遇率最高的国家，51%的受访者承认有过婚外情。

Thai media attribute this to the culturally embedded practice of Mia Noi, where men maintain unofficial secondary partners, a tradition that tacitly permits infidelity.
泰国媒体将此归因于文化中根深蒂固的“小妻”（Mia Noi）习俗，即男性保留非正式的二房，这种传统默许了不忠行为。

Additionally, Thailand’s robust sex industry, closely tied to its tourism sector, lowers the social and economic barriers to extramarital relationships.
此外，泰国蓬勃发展的性产业与其旅游业紧密相连，降低了婚外关系的社会和经济门槛。

These factors contribute to a permissive atmosphere around male-female relationships, where infidelity is often normalized or even economically incentivized[^2].
这些因素共同营造了一种对男女关系放任自流的氛围，外遇往往被正常化，甚至在经济上受到鼓励。

The world we live in, the media we consume daily, and the pervasive atmosphere of promiscuity all influence us - putting believers, Christian families, and the church of Christ at increased risk.
我们所生活的世界、每天接触的媒体以及无处不在的放荡氛围都在影响着我们——使信徒、基督徒家庭和基督的教会面临越来越大的风险。

#### 1. Sin begins in the heart, it's not merely in the act / 1. 罪始于内心，而不仅仅在于行为本身 (5:27-28)

##### a. Adultery is not merely an outward act.. (v27) / a. 奸淫不仅仅是外在的行为……（第27节）

>27 “You have heard that it was said, ‘Do not commit adultery.’
27「你们听见有话说：『不可奸淫。』

We find the law against adultery in the Ten Commandments (Exodus 20:14 and Deut 5:18), where we read “you shall not commit adultery”.
我们在十诫中找到了禁止奸淫的律法（出埃及记 20:14；申命记 5:18），经上写着：“不可奸淫。”（新标点和合本）

The Hebrew term for ‘adultery, נָאַף‎ (nā·ʾǎp̄) means to having “sexual intercourse with someone other than a spouse” (Swanson 1997, §5537).
希伯来语中的“奸淫”（נָאַף‎，nā·ʾǎp̄）意思是“与配偶以外的人发生性关系”（Swanson 1997, §5537）。

Adultery therefore “refers to a consensual sexual relationship between a married person and someone other than his or her spouse”[^3](Barry et al. (2016, § Matt 5:27).
因此，奸淫“指的是已婚人士与配偶以外的人之间自愿发生的性关系”（Barry等 2016）。

The Mosaic Law commanded that if a man or a woman was found to commit adultery with his neighbor’s wife, ...he must be put to death (Lev 20:10).
摩西律法规定，如果发现男人或女人与邻舍的妻子行淫，“奸夫淫妇都必被治死”（利未记 20:10，新标点和合本）。

“In several countries today, adultery remains a capital offense under laws influenced by conservative interpretations of Islamic Sharia. Nations such as Iran, Saudi Arabia, Somalia, and parts of Nigeria and Pakistan retain legal frameworks where adultery—especially by married individuals—can be punished by death, often by stoning.[^4]
在今天的一些国家，受保守伊斯兰教法影响的法律仍然将奸淫定为死罪。伊朗、沙特阿拉伯、索马里以及尼日利亚和巴基斯坦的部分地区等国家保留了法律框架，奸淫（特别是已婚者）可被处以死刑，通常是被石刑打死。

In Jesus' day, under Roman law, people were no longer put to death for adultery.
在耶稣时代的罗马法律下，人们不再因奸淫而被处死。

Yet this Mosaic stipulations communicated the seriousness of adultery in God's eyes.
然而，摩西律法的这些规定传达了在神眼中奸淫的严重性。

What however was the problem with the Jewish leaders' interpretation and teaching the law against adultery?
然而，犹太领袖对禁止奸淫律法的解释和教导出了什么问题？

Just as with the command against murder, the Pharisees and the teachers of the law conveniently narrowed the interpretation of the law to a mere physical act, overlooking the issue of lust in the heart.
就像对待禁止杀人的诫命一样，法利赛人和文士顺手将律法的解释缩小为仅仅是肢体上的行为，忽略了心中动淫念的问题。

John Stott (1985, 87 § Matt 5:27) explains:
斯托得（John Stott 1985, 87）解释道：

> “Once again the rabbis were attempting to limit the scope of the commandment you shall not commit adultery.
> “拉比们再次试图限制‘不可奸淫’这条诫命的适用范围。
> 
> Although the sin of desiring another man’s wife is included in the tenth commandment against covetousness, they evidently found it more comfortable to ignore this.
> 虽然在第十条关于禁止贪恋的诫命里，已经包含了妄想他人妻子的罪，但他们显然觉得忽略这一点更为舒适。
> 
> In their view, they kept the seventh commandment, provided that they avoided the act of adultery itself.
> 在他们看来，只要避免了奸淫的行为本身，他们就遵守了第七条诫命。
> 
> They thus gave a conveniently narrow definition of sexual sin and a conveniently broad definition of sexual purity” (Stott 1985, 87 § Matt 5:27).
> 他们因此给性罪恶下了一个方便的狭隘定义，并给性纯洁下了一个方便的宽泛定义。”（斯托得 1985, 87）

##### b. Adultery takes place in the heart (v28) / b. 奸淫发生在心里（第28节）

Jesus therefore, carefully and systematically exposes the sin in the human heart.
因此，耶稣十分细致、系统地揭露了人心中的罪。

For just as there is **anger** beneath the sin of murder (v21-26), so we will see there is **lust** beneath the sin of adultery (vs 27-30), and “a **hard heart**” beneath divorce (verses 31-32).
因为正如杀人的罪之下有**怒气**（21-26节），我们也会看到奸淫的罪之下有**淫念/情欲**（27-30节），而在离婚之下有着“**心硬**”（31-32节）。

28 But I tell you that anyone who **looks** at a woman lustfully has already committed adultery with her in his heart.
28只是我告诉你们，凡看见妇女就动淫念的，这人心里已经与她犯奸淫了。（新标点和合本）

Notice how Jesus shifts the focus from the act of committing, to the process of looking, lusting and committing adultery in the heart.
请注意耶稣如何将焦点从实施行为，转向看、动淫念以及在心里犯奸淫的过程。

The Greek *blepo* simply means ‘to see, to notice, to glance at" someone (LN 1996, 277 §24.7).
希腊语 *blepo* 仅仅意味着“看见、注意到、瞥一眼”某人。

However the phrase "everyone who is looking" (*pas ho blepon*), suggests a deliberate, sustained, or cultivating gaze rather than a merely incidental glance (Hagner 1993, 135; Davies and Allison 1988, 527).
然而，“凡看见/凝视的”（*pas ho blepon*）这一短语，指的是一种蓄意、持续或滋养的凝视，而不是仅仅无意的看一眼。

Such purposeful look with the purpose of “of lusting" (*pros to epithymesai auten*) turns into adultery in the heart [^5].
这种带着“动淫念”（*pros to epithymesai auten*）目的的蓄意凝视，就演变成了心里的奸淫。

We live in a world where it’s challenging to go through even a single day without being bombarded with images or media that are intentionally crafted to stimulate us sexually, or create sexual longing or desire.
我们生活在一个极具挑战的世界里，甚至一天之中不被那些旨在刺激性欲望或引发性渴望的图像和媒体轰炸都很难。

Yet Jesus, is not here addressing such accidental attraction, but rather when an incidental look, turns into a purposeful and settled gaze - longing holds potential to become adultery in the heart.
然而耶稣在这里并不是针对那种无意的吸引，而是指当无意的一瞥演变成有目的、固定的凝视时——这种渴望就在心里构成了奸淫。

Jesus therefore exposes the covetous desires that lies beneath adultery, and demonstrates that sin begins in the heart before it manifests itself in outward action (Davies and Allison 1988, 526-28; Carson 2010, 146-47).
因此，耶稣揭露了潜藏在奸淫背后的贪恋欲望，并表明罪在表现为外在行为之前就已经始于内心。

Being a disciple of Christ, and seeking God's kingdom and his righteousness, therefore is not merely about “behavioral management”, or external obedience - instead it is about being transformed from the inside out - from within the heart.
因此，作为基督的门徒并寻求神的国和神的义，不仅仅是关于“行为管理”或外在的服从，而是关于由内而外的生命翻转——从心里开始的翻转。

##### c. The Example of David and Bathsheba (2 Sam 11:2-4) / c. 大卫与拔示巴的鉴戒（撒下 11:2-4）

Bible commentator RT France (2007, 204 § Matt 5:27-30) notes that the GK OT (LXX) use the same word *epithymeo* (lust) in 2 Sam 11:2-4 when David looked with lust at Bathsheba, when he saw her half naked while bathing on her roof.
圣经注释家 RT France 注解道，七十士译本在撒母耳记下 11:2-4 中大卫在房顶上看拔示巴沐浴并动淫念时，使用了同一个词 *epithymeo*（贪恋/动淫念）。

To bathe on the roof of a house in the coolness of the evening was a common practice at that time, and culturally David ought to have given her privacy, especially in view that Bathsheba took a ritual bath of cleansing after her monthly period (2 Sam 11:2-5).
傍晚在屋顶借着凉风沐浴是当时常见的习俗，从文化习惯上讲，大卫本应避开给她隐私，特别是考虑到拔示巴是在月经后进行律法洁净的沐浴。

It's very sobering to see how David, even as a man of God, transfixed by what he saw, casts off all restraint, and ignores the warning of his servant that Bathsheba was the wife of Uriah.
非常令人警惕的是，大卫即使作为属神的人，被眼所见迷住后，竟抛开了一切约束，甚至无视仆人关于拔示巴是乌利亚妻子的提醒。

Yet with the hook of lust firmly swallowed, David ordered to get her, so that he could get what he wanted.
然而吞下了欲望的毒钩后，大卫下令去把她接来，以便得到他想要的东西。

Any sympathy we might have for David for what initially seemed like an accidental glance quickly dissipates as we witness how he exploits his position of power, influence, and wealth to satisfy his desires and then conceal them.
我们对大卫最初看似无意一瞥的任何同情，在他利用权力、影响力和财富来满足欲望并加以掩盖时迅速烟消云散。

From a woman's perspective you have to see the horror, injustice of turning someone created in the image of God as merely an object for satisfaction.
从女性的角度来看，你必须看到将一个按神的形象受造的人仅仅降格为满足私欲的工具是多么恐怖和不公。

At the same time you wonder whether Bathsheba could have been more discreet in protecting her privacy and not creating a stumbling block for those who might happen to gaze upon her.
同时，你也会想拔示巴是否本可以更加谨慎地保护自己的隐私，而不给可能看到她的人留下绊脚石。

Scripture gives us no indication that Bathsheba resisted or protested the King's advances, which leaves us to wonder if she had been a willing participant in the adultery?
圣经没有给出任何拔示巴反抗或抗议国王接近的暗示，这让我们想知道她是否也是奸淫的主动参与者？

We could further speculate that her husband’s prolonged absence may have made her both emotionally and physically vulnerable to the attention and advances of a man who made her feel special, beautiful, or desirable.
我们可以进一步推测，她丈夫长期的在外缺席，可能使她在情感和生理上都变得脆弱，容易被一个让她感到特别、美丽或受渴望的男人的关怀和追求所动摇。

The fact that he was a man of means and possibilities may have caused her to cast off her restraints as well.
大卫是一个有地位和能力的人，这也可能促使她放下了防备。

The point I am making is that whether due to lust that enters through the eyes of man, or whether caused by loneliness, emotional neglect, or romantic longing - both men and women are vulnerable to this sin of adultery which might strike at your heart unexpectedly.
我想表达的是，无论是通过男人的眼睛进入的色欲，还是出于孤独、情感忽视或浪漫渴望——男女都容易受到这种奸淫之罪的伤害，它可能会出其不意地击中你的心。

Jesus was frustrated with the Pharisees' hypocritical handling of the adultery law, because the OT provided ample teaching against such lustful longing.
耶稣对法利赛人假冒伪善地处理奸淫律法感到愤慨，因为旧约提供了大量反对这种情欲渴望的教导。

To begin with the Tenth Commandment very clearly deals with this issue of covetousness when it says "You shall not covet your neighbor’s house. You shall not covet your neighbor’s wife" (Exod 20:17).
首先，第十条诫命非常明确地处理了贪恋的问题，经上说：“不可贪恋人的房屋；也不可贪恋人的妻子”（出埃及记 20:17，新标点和合本）。

An Israelite woman certainly would have understood the law as "you should not covet your neighbor's husband".
以色列女性当然也会将这条律法理解为“不可贪恋邻舍的丈夫”。

The path to covetousness for a woman might look different than for a man - perhaps longing for connection, intimacy, being listened to, prospect of a good income and home etc... Yet it still enters the heart through what you see or hear, and is still covetous lust.
女性走向贪恋的路径可能与男性不同——也许是渴望连接、亲密感、被倾听、良好的收入和家庭前景等……然而它仍然通过你所见所闻进入内心，依然是贪恋的情欲。

The OT prophet Job is a good example for us as men. He remained vigilant by saying "I made a covenant with my eyes not to look lustfully at a girl" (Job 31:1 NIV).
旧约先知约伯对我们男性是一个极好的榜样。他警惕地说：“我与眼睛立约，怎能瞻仰处女呢？”（约伯记 31:1，新标点和合本）

Solomon, perhaps with his father David's sin in mind, urged his son not to lust in his heart after the beauty of the immoral woman, nor to be captivated by her eyes, or by her smooth tongue (Prov 6:24-25).
所罗门也许记着他父亲大卫的罪，敦促他的儿子心内不要恋慕恶妇的美色，也不要被她的眼目或谄媚的舌头勾引（箴言 6:24-25，新标点和合本）。

##### d. Jesus convicts us all as sexual sinners (v28) / d. 耶稣定罪我们所有人都是性罪人（第28节）

Have a look again at v 28:
让我们再看一遍第28节：

28 But I tell you that anyone who looks at a woman lustfully **has already** committed adultery with her in his heart.
28只是我告诉你们，凡看见妇女就动淫念的，这人心里已经与她犯奸淫了。（新标点和合本）

Notice the sense of surprise and shock here to discover that you may have already committed adultery in your heart...(v28)
请注意这里的震惊与吃惊，发现自己可能已经在心里犯了奸淫……（第28节）

When Jesus speaks of the heart (*kardia*), he is figuratively referring to our inner-life with all its secret thoughts, feelings, motives and desires that only you and God can see (LN 1996, 320 § 26.3).
当耶稣说到“心”（*kardia*）时，祂比喻性地指的是我们的内心生活，包括只有你和神才能看到的隐秘思想、情感、动机和欲望。

Jesus' words bring home a shocking realization, that we are all sexual sinners, that we have, and continue to sin with lustful eyes, and covetous hearts.
耶稣的话带给我们一个震撼的认识：我们都是性罪人，我们曾经并继续用有淫念的眼睛和贪恋的心犯罪。

So Jesus here is not merely convicting the Pharisees, but he convicts all of us as sexual sinners.
所以耶稣在这里不仅是在定法利赛人的罪，更是定我们所有人的罪，显明我们都是性罪人。

Jon Courson, here notes...
寇森（Jon Courson）在这里注解道：

> “We have all looked with lust in our heart, thus we’re all adulterers. Truly, there is none righteous, no not one” (Courson 2003, 29 § Matt 5:27).
> “我们心里都有过动淫念的凝视，因此我们都是奸淫者。真实的，没有义人，连一个也没有。”（寇森 2003, 29）

Jesus’ purpose in Matthew 5 was to use the Law of God to reveal, and indeed to convict all of us who are convinced of our own righteousness, that our hearts are desperately wicked and in need of God’s work of grace and restoration.
耶稣在马太福音第5章的目的是借着神的律法揭示并定罪我们所有自以为义的人，显明我们的心极其邪恶，需要神恩典与恢复的工作。

#### 2. Sin in the heart demands decisive repentance / 2. 内心的罪需要果断的悔改 (5:29-30)

As we move to vv. 29-30, notice how Jesus underlines the seriousness of this sin and the importance of timely and decisive action.
当我们看第29-30节时，请注意耶稣如何强调这种罪的严重性，以及及时采取果断行动的重要性。

>29 If your right eye causes you to sin, gouge it out and throw it away. It is better for you to lose one part of your body than for your whole body to be thrown into hell. 30 And if your right hand causes you to sin, cut it off and throw it away. It is better for you to lose one part of your body than for your whole body to go into hell.
29若是你的右眼叫你跌倒，就剜出来丢掉，宁可失去百体中的一体，不叫全身丢在地狱里。30若是右手叫你跌倒，就砍下来丢掉，宁可失去百体中的一体，不叫全身下入地狱。」（新标点和合本）

It goes without saying that Jesus doesn’t actually want us to physically gouge out our eye for lust or cut off our hand for touching what’s forbidden.[^6]
不用说，耶稣并不是真要我们在肢体上剜出眼睛或砍下右手。

His point is that our covetous and lustful desires are so serious that it's worth the considerable cost of stopping it both at the source and its outflow into action [^7].
祂的要旨是，我们的贪恋和情欲是如此严重，以至于值得付出巨大的代价在源头和行为输出上将其切断。

##### a. The self-reinforcing cycle of sin - explained (v29-30) / a. 罪自我强化循环的解析（第29-30节）

What is particularly striking is that Jesus does not simply command us to stop lusting in our heart.
特别令人震撼的是，耶稣不仅仅是命令我们停止在心里动淫念。

Instead, He specifically calls us to deal decisively with both the eye and the hand.
相反，祂特别呼召我们果断处理眼睛和手。

..If your right eye causes you to stumble, gouge it out and throw it away..(v29)
……若是你的右眼叫你跌倒，就剜出来丢掉……（第29节）

..And if your right hand causes you to stumble, cut it off and throw it away...(v30)
……若是右手叫你跌倒，就砍下来丢掉……（第30节）

He addresses both the inflow into the heart and the outflow from the heart.
祂同时回应了进入内心的输入和从内心出来的输出。

Why? A self-reinforcing cycle of sin can develop and continue for months or even years unless it is decisively broken. Notice the pattern in the diagram:
为什么？因为罪会形成自我强化的循环，如果不果断斩断，可能会持续数月甚至数年。请注意图表中的模式：


The eye feeds the heart, and the heart directs the eye toward what it desires.
眼睛滋养内心，内心又指引眼睛看向它所渴望的东西。

Those desires then move the hand to act, bringing a moment of satisfaction.
这些欲望随后驱动手去行动，带来短暂的满足。

Yet that satisfaction does not extinguish the desire; it strengthens it.
然而，这种满足并没有熄灭欲望，反而加强了欲望。

The stronger desire seeks further stimulation through the eye, and the cycle begins again with increasing intensity.
更强烈的欲望借着眼睛寻找进一步的刺激，循环再次开始，强度不断递增。

This helps explain why Jesus speaks so forcefully about the eye and the hand.
这解释了为什么耶稣对眼睛和手讲得如此有力。

He is teaching us as his disciples to take radical action against anything that either fuels sinful desires at their source or enables its expression in practice.
祂是在教导我们作为门徒，要采取激进的行动抗衡任何在源头滋养罪恶欲望或在实践中助长其表达的事物。

##### b. The self-reinforcing cycle of sin - illustrated / b. 罪自我强化循环的比喻

To illustrate, imagine a man (like myself) entering midlife who has long dreamed of owning a powerful and expensive Harley Davidson motorcycle.
为了说明这一点，想象一个步入中年的男人（像我一样），长期梦想拥有一台马力强大且昂贵的哈雷戴维森摩托车。

At first, the desire is relatively small. He begins watching videos, reading reviews, and browsing photos online. The more attention he gives it, the more the desire grows.
起初，这个欲望相对较小。他开始在网上看视频、读测评、浏览照片。他给予的关注越多，欲望就增长得越厉害。

Before long, he visits a dealership, sits on the motorcycle, and perhaps takes it for a test ride.
不久，他去到车行，坐在摩托车上，甚至试驾了一圈。

These experiences bring a measure of satisfaction, but they also deepen his longing.
这些体验带来了一定程度的满足，但也加深了他的渴望。

Gradually, his thoughts, conversations, time, and money begin to revolve around acquiring what his heart desires.
渐渐地，他的思想、谈话、时间和金钱开始围绕着获取他内心所渴望的东西运转。

If he wants to break the hold that this desire has on his heart and mind, he must cut off both the sources that feed it and the experiences that continually reinforce it.
如果他想斩断这种欲望对他内心和思想的掌控，他就必须切断滋养它的源头和不断强化它的体验。

##### c. The self-reinforcing cycle of sin - applied / c. 罪自我强化循环的应用

Imagine you reconnect with an old friend or former boyfriend/girlfriend on social media.
想象你在社交媒体上与一位老朋友或前男友/前女友重新联系上。

At first, it seems completely innocent. You look at a few photos, read some updates, and wonder how life has turned out for them. But the more attention you give them, the more often they occupy your thoughts.
起初，这似乎完全是无害的。你看几张照片，读几条近况，好奇他们生活得怎样。但你给予的关注越多，他们就越频繁地占据你的思想。

Before long, you find yourself checking their profile regularly, looking forward to new posts or messages, and imagining what life might have been like if things had turned out differently.
不久，你发现自己定期查看他们的主页，期待新的动态或信息，并想象如果当年情况不同，生活会是什么样子。

These interactions bring a measure of satisfaction, but they also deepen the emotional attachment.
这些互动带来了一定程度的满足，但也加深了情感上的依恋。

Gradually, your thoughts, emotions, and attention become increasingly focused on that person.
渐渐地，你的思想、情感和注意力越来越集中在那个人身上。

What began as curiosity has grown into something far more powerful longing.
始于好奇的东西已经演变成了强大得多的渴望。

This is why Jesus calls for radical action. Sin must be confronted both at its source and at its point of expression.
这就是为什么耶稣呼召我们采取激进的行动。罪必须在其源头和表达点同时受到遏制。

**Whatever feeds sinful desire and whatever gives it opportunity to grow must be dealt with decisively[^8].**
**凡滋养罪恶欲望的事物以及凡赋予其增长机会的事物，都必须果断处理。**

> “The threat of lust is so strong and its dangers so great that a price comparable to the removal of eye or hand is not too great to pay as one seeks freedom from it... (Thomas 1998, §4624; Hagner 1993, 120; Stagg 1969, 109)
> “情欲的威胁如此强烈，其危险如此巨大，因此在寻求从中获得自由时，付出与挖眼断手相当的代价也不算太大……”（托马斯 1998; 汉格纳 1993, 120; 斯塔格 1969, 109）

This might mean that you are reminded this morning that there might be a person that you are giving too much thought to, or allowing secret longing.
这可能意味着今天早上提醒你，可能有一个人你给予了过多的心思，或者允许了隐秘的渴望。

#### Summary and Conclusion / 总结与结语

Friends, firstly we have been reminded that
弟兄姊妹们，首先我们被提醒：

##### Sin begins in the heart, not merely in the act (Matt 5:27–28) / 罪始于内心，而不仅仅在于行为本身（太 5:27–28）

The religious leaders conveniently restricted the Seventh Commandment ("Do not commit adultery") to physical acts of illicit intercourse.
宗教领袖顺手将第七条诫命（“不可奸淫”）局限于非法性行为的肢体动作。

Jesus rejects this narrow definition, establishing that kingdom righteousness requires transformed motives and desires rather than superficial behavior management.
耶稣拒绝了这种狭隘的定义，确立了天国的公义需要动机和欲望的翻转，而不是表面上的行为管理。

Jesus does not condemn us for the accidental glance or natural visual attraction, but warns that deliberate, sustained gaze cultivated for the explicit purpose of self-gratifying lust is already adultery.
耶稣并没有因为无意的一瞥或自然的视觉吸引而定我们的罪，而是警告说，出于自私满足情欲目的而蓄意滋养的持续凝视，本身就已经是奸淫。

Such adultery of the heart is rooted in coveting what belongs to someone else.
这种心里的奸淫根源于贪恋属于别人的东西。

Lust objectifies an image-bearer created to be loved and honored, reducing them to an object to be consumed.
情欲物化了按神形象受造、本该被爱和尊重的形象承载者，将其降格为被消费的对象。

Secondly we have seen that
其次，我们看到：

##### Sin in the heart demands decisive repentance (Matt 5:29–30) / 内心的罪需要果断的悔改（太 5:29–30）

Jesus uses the shocking hyperbole—gouging out an eye or cutting off a hand—not to command physical self-mutilation, but to demand radical action against whatever traps or ensnares us (*skandalizo*).
耶稣使用了震撼的比喻——剜出眼睛或砍下手——不是要命令身体的自残，而是要求对任何陷害或网罗我们的事物（*skandalizo*）采取激进的行动。

By addressing both the eye (what enters the heart) and the hand (what proceeds from the heart), Jesus calls us as His disciples to eliminate the environments, habits, media, and relationships that feed wrongful desires before they cause spiritual destruction.
借着同时回应眼睛（进入内心的东西）和手（从内心出来的东西），耶稣呼召我们作为祂的门徒，在错误欲望造成属灵毁灭之前，切断滋养它们的环境、习惯、媒体和关系。

##### Application: The Path of Hope / 应用：盼望之路

Thirdly Jesus's teaching here prompts us to ask..
第三，耶稣在这里的教导促使我们思考：

What then is the path of hope and restoration in the Sermon on the Mount?
那么，在登山宝训中，盼望与恢复之路究竟是什么？

Whether our struggle is lust, covetousness, or some other misplaced desire, none of us can stand before these words and claim innocence.
无论我们的挣扎是情欲、贪恋还是其他错位的欲望，我们没有人能站在这些话语面前自称无辜。

Jesus exposes the hidden motives of our hearts not to leave us crushed under moralistic condemnation or hopeless self-effort.
耶稣揭露我们内心的隐秘动机，不是为了让我们在律法主义的定罪或绝望的自我努力下崩溃。

Instead there is much hope even within the Sermon on the Mount.
相反，即使在登山宝训中，也有着极大的盼望。

Let's return to Jesus' opening words in the Beatitudes:
让我们回到耶稣在八福中的开篇宣告：

> “Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven. Blessed are those who mourn, for they will be comforted... Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they will be filled” (Matt 5:3-4, 6 CUVNP).
> “虚心的人有福了！因为天国是他们的。哀恸的人有福了！因为他们必得安慰。……饥渴慕义的人有福了！因为他们必得饱足。”（太 5:3-4, 6，新标点和合本）

Jesus' teaching this morning has exposed the sin in every one of our hearts, yet he does not expose our sin in order to drive us away from Him. He exposes our sin in order to draw us to Him.
今天早上耶稣的教导揭露了我们每个人心里的罪，然而祂揭露我们的罪不是为了把我们从祂身边赶走，祂揭露我们的罪是为了把我们吸引到祂面前。

From the very beginning of the Sermon on the Mount, He invites us to come as one - poor in spirit, mourning over our sin, and hungry and thirsty for His righteousness - the righteousness that we do not possess in ourselves.
从登山宝训的最开头，祂就邀请我们像这样来到祂面前——心里虚心，为罪哀恸，饥渴慕义——慕那我们在自己里面并不拥有的公义。

And to those of us who come to Him with repentant faith, Jesus makes wonderful promises - the kingdom will belong to us, we will be comforted, and we indeed will be filled or clothed with Christ's righteousness.
对我们这些带着悔改信心来到祂面前的人，耶稣给出了美好的应许——天国属于我们，我们必得安慰，我们必得饱足并披戴基督的公义。

Therefore, our hope is not found in our ability to conquer every sinful desire by our own strength.
因此，我们的盼望不在于靠自己的力量战胜每一个罪恶欲望的能力。

Our hope is found in Christ, who forgives sinners, who comforts the broken-hearted, and who gives us His righteousness through faith in Him.
我们的盼望存在于基督里面，祂赦免罪人，安慰伤心的人，并借着对祂的信心将祂的公义赐给我们。

So if Jesus' words have convicted you this morning, do not run from Him. Run to Him.
所以，如果今天早上耶稣的话语责备了你，不要逃离祂，要奔向祂。

Confess your sin, seek His mercy, and trust His grace.
承认你的罪，寻求祂的怜悯，信靠祂的恩典。

The One who exposes the sin of the heart is also the One who heals and transforms the heart.
那揭露人心之罪的，正是那医治并翻转人心的主。

And as we look to the closing words of the Sermon on the Mount, we hear Jesus say:
当我们看到登山宝训的结束语时，我们听到耶稣说：

> “Everyone who hears these words of mine and puts them into practice is like a wise man who built his house on the rock” (Matt 7:24 CUVNP).
> “所以，凡听见我这话就去行的，好比一个聪明人，把房子盖在磐石上。”（太 7:24，新标点和合本）

Instead of fixing our eyes on what we cannot have, Jesus calls us to fix our eyes on Him.
耶稣呼召我们转眼仰望祂，而不是将目光盯在我们不能拥有的事物上。

Instead of allowing our hearts to be shaped by sinful desires, He calls us to build our lives upon His Word.
祂呼召我们将生命建造在祂的话语上，而不是允许我们的心被罪恶的欲望塑造。

As we come to Christ in faith and humility, listening to His voice, feeding on His truth, and putting His teaching into practice, the Holy Spirit gradually transforms our hearts and minds.
当我们带着信心和谦卑来到基督面前，倾听祂的声音，领受祂的真理，并将祂的教导付诸实践时，圣灵会逐渐翻转我们的心灵和思想。

Old desires begin to lose their grip, and new desires begin to take their place. Our love for Christ grows stronger than our love for sin.
旧的欲望开始失去掌控，新的欲望开始取而代之。我们对基督的爱变得比对罪的爱更强。

This is the hope of the Sermon on the Mount.
这就是登山宝训的盼望。

Jesus does not merely expose the sin within us; He also provides the foundation upon which we can build a new life.
耶稣不仅揭露了我们里面的罪，祂还提供了我们可以据以建造新生命的根基。

The One who reveals the sickness of the heart is also the One who heals it.
那揭示人心疾病的，正是那医治人心的主。

And as we build our lives upon Him, He graciously transforms us into the very people He calls us to be. Amen.
当我们把生命建造在祂上面时，祂凭恩典将我们翻转成为祂呼召我们成为的人。阿们。
