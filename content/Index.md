# The Sermon on the Mount Collection: Kingdom Character, Witness, and Heart Ethics

> *"You are the salt of the earth... You are the light of the world. A city set on a hill cannot be hidden."* — **Matthew 5:13–14**

Welcome to **The Sermon on the Mount Collection**. This repository serves as a central jump site and narrative roadmap for the Matthew 5 sermon series preached at **Lakeside International Church (LIC)** in 2026.

---

## 📖 The Narrative Arc of Matthew 5

The Sermon on the Mount represents Jesus’ definitive manifesto on citizenship in the Kingdom of Heaven. Rather than offering isolated moral maxims, Jesus lays out a tightly integrated, progressive framework for heart transformation.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          THE NARRATIVE FLOW                             │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. KINGDOM CHARACTER (Matt 5:3–12)                                      │
│    Internal transformation through the Beatitudes                       │
│    └─► Poor in Spirit ──► Mourning ──► Meekness ──► Hungering           │
│                                                                         │
│ 2. KINGDOM WITNESS (Matt 5:13–16)                                       │
│    Public impact of transformed citizens                                │
│    ├─► Salt of the Earth (Moral Preservation & Spiritual Flavor)        │
│    └─► Light of the World (Visible Good Deeds for God's Glory)          │
│                                                                         │
│ 3. KINGDOM ETHICS (Matt 5:21–32)                                        │
│    Radical heart-level righteousness replacing legalistic loopholes     │
│    ├─► Anger & Bitterness ──► Urgent Reconciliation (5:21–26)           │
│    ├─► Lust & Covetousness ──► Radical Mortification of Sin (5:27–30)   │
│    └─► Marital Breakdown   ──► Covenant Faithfulness (5:31–32)          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Kingdom Character: The Beatitudes (Matt 5:3–12)
Jesus begins not with commands, but with blessings (*macarisms*). The Beatitudes describe the inner heart dispositions of kingdom citizens:
* **The Needy & Merciful Heart**: Those who are *poor in spirit* (v.3) recognize spiritual bankruptcy, producing *mercy* toward others (v.7).
* **The Repentant & Pure Heart**: Those who *mourn over sin* (v.4) are purified within, becoming *pure in heart* (v.8).
* **The Humble & Peacemaking Heart**: The *meek* (v.5) yield self-will to become active *peacemakers* (v.9).
* **The Righteous & Enduring Heart**: Those who *hunger for righteousness* (v.6) endure *persecution* for Christ's sake (v.10–12).

### 2. Kingdom Witness: Salt and Light (Matt 5:13–16)
Transformed character flows into public testimony:
* **Salt of the Earth (v.13)**: Restraining moral decay in society while enriching the world with gospel truth. Losing "saltiness" means compromising until witness becomes worthless.
* **Light of the World (v.14–16)**: Functioning as a city set on a hill. Authentic faith shines through good deeds (*kalos*) that direct praise to the Heavenly Father.

### 3. Kingdom Ethics: Heart Transformation (Matt 5:21–32)
Exposing legalistic loopholes, Jesus introduces the radical formula: *"You have heard that it was said... but I tell you."*
* **Anger & Reconciliation (v.21–26)**: Murder originates in unresolved resentment and contemptuous insults (*Raca*). Urgent reconciliation must precede worship.
* **Lust & Purity (v.27–30)**: Adultery begins in intentional lust. Jesus commands radical, decisive mortification of sin to sever temptation at its root.
* **Marriage & Covenant Faithfulness (v.31–32)**: Rebutting casual divorce allowances, Jesus upholds God's creation design of lifelong marital fidelity.
