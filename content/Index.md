# 🏔️ The Sermon on the Mount
> *"You are the salt of the earth... You are the light of the world. A city set on a hill cannot be hidden."* — **Matthew 5:13–14**

Welcome to **The Sermon on the Mount Collection**, a digital sermon repository and study guide covering Jesus’ foundational teachings in **Matthew 5:13–32**, preached at Lakeside International Church (LIC).

---

## 🗺️ Series Overview & Narrative Arc

Jesus’ message in Matthew 5 is not a disconnected set of rules, but a progressive journey of heart transformation and kingdom witness:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      MATTHEW 5 NARRATIVE FLOW                           │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. KINGDOM WITNESS (Matt 5:13–16)                                       │
│    Public identity of transformed believers                             │
│    ├─► Salt of the Earth — Moral preservation & spiritual flavor       │
│    └─► Light of the World — Visible good deeds glorifying the Father   │
│                                                                         │
│ 2. KINGDOM ETHICS (Matt 5:21–32)                                        │
│    Radical heart-level righteousness over legalistic externalism       │
│    ├─► Anger & Bitterness ──► Immediate Reconciliation (5:21–26)        │
│    ├─► Lust & Adultery   ──► Radical Mortification of Sin (5:27–30)    │
│    └─► Marital Breakdown ──► Covenant Faithfulness (5:31–32)            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
