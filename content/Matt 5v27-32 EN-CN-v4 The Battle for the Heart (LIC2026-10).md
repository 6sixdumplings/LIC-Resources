##### a. Adultery is not merely an outward act 奸淫不仅仅是外在的行为（第27节）

> 27 “You have heard that it was said, ‘Do not commit adultery.’
> 27「你们听见有话说：『不可奸淫。』

We find the law against adultery in the Ten Commandments (Exodus 20:14 and Deut 5:18), where we read “you shall not commit adultery”.
我们在十诫中找到了禁止奸淫的律法（出埃及记 20:14 和 申命记 5:18），经上写着：“不可奸淫。”

The Hebrew term for ‘adultery’, נָאַף‎ (nā·ʾǎp̄), means having “sexual intercourse with someone other than a spouse” (Swanson 1997, §5537).
希伯来语中的“奸淫”（נָאַף, nā·ʾǎp̄）意思是“与配偶以外的人发生性行为”（Swanson 1997, §5537）。

Adultery therefore “refers to a consensual sexual relationship between a married person and someone other than his or her spouse”[^3] (Barry et al. 2016, § Matt 5:27).
因此，奸淫“是指已婚人士与配偶以外的人之间自愿发生的性关系”[^3]（Barry et al. 2016, § Matt 5:27）。

The Mosaic Law commanded that if a man or a woman was found to commit adultery with his neighbor’s wife, ...he must be put to death (Lev 20:10).
摩西律法规定，若有人与邻舍的妻行淫，奸夫淫妇都必被治死（利未记 20:10）。

In several countries today, adultery remains a capital offense under laws influenced by conservative interpretations of Islamic Sharia.
在今天的一些国家，在受保守伊斯兰教法影响的法律下，奸淫仍属于死罪。

Nations such as Iran, Saudi Arabia, Somalia, and parts of Nigeria and Pakistan retain legal frameworks where adultery—especially by married individuals—can be punished by death, often by stoning.[^4]
伊朗、沙特阿拉伯、索马里以及尼日利亚和巴基斯坦部分地区等国家保留了法律框架，其中奸淫行为——尤其是已婚者的奸淫——可被判处死刑，通常是石刑。[^4]

In Jesus' day, under Roman law, people were no longer put to death for adultery.
在耶稣时代，在罗马法律下，人们不再因奸淫而被处死。

Yet these Mosaic stipulations communicated the seriousness of adultery in God's eyes.
然而，摩西律法的这些规定传达了在神眼中奸淫罪的严厉性。

What however was the problem with the Jewish leaders' interpretation and teaching of the law against adultery?
然而，犹太领袖对禁止奸淫律法的解释和教导出了什么问题？

Just as with the command against murder, the Pharisees and the teachers of the law conveniently narrowed the interpretation of the law to a mere physical act, overlooking the issue of lust in the heart.
正如对待禁止杀人的诫命一样，法利赛人和文士投机取巧地将律法的解释狭隘化为仅仅是外在的肢体行为，从而忽视了内心邪念的问题。

John Stott (1985, 87 § Matt 5:27) explains:
约翰·斯托特（John Stott 1985, 87 § Matt 5:27）解释道：

> “Once again the rabbis were attempting to limit the scope of the commandment you shall not commit adultery.
> “拉比们再次试图限制‘不可奸淫’这条诫命的适用范围。
> 
> Although the sin of desiring another man’s wife is included in the tenth commandment against covetousness, they evidently found it more comfortable to ignore this.
> 虽然贪恋他人妻子的罪已被包含在第十条关于禁止贪婪的诫命之中，但他们显然觉得忽略这一点更为舒适。
> 
> In their view, they ...kept the seventh commandment, provided that they avoided the act of adultery itself.
> 在他们看来，只要避免了奸淫的行为本身，他们就是……遵守了第七条诫命。
> 
> They thus gave a conveniently narrow definition of sexual sin and a conveniently broad definition of sexual purity” (Stott 1985, 87 § Matt 5:27).
> 他们由此给出了一个便利的狭隘性犯罪定义，以及一个便利的宽泛性洁净定义。”（Stott 1985, 87 § Matt 5:27）

##### b. Adultery takes place in the heart 奸淫发生在心里（第28节）

Jesus therefore, carefully and systematically exposes the sin in the human heart.
因此，耶稣仔细而系统地揭露了人心中的罪。

For just as there is **anger** beneath the sin of murder (vv. 21-26), so we will see there is **lust** beneath the sin of adultery (vv. 27-30), and “a **hard heart**” beneath divorce (vv. 31-32).
因为正如杀人罪的背后是**怒气**（第21-26节），我们同样看到奸淫罪的背后是**情欲**（第27-30节），而离婚的背后是“**硬心**”（第31-32节）。

> 28 But I tell you that anyone who **looks** at a woman lustfully has already committed adultery with her in his heart.
> 28只是我告诉你们，凡看见妇女就动淫念的，这人心里已经与她犯奸淫了。

Notice how Jesus shifts the focus from the act of committing, to the process of looking, lusting and committing adultery in the heart.
请注意耶稣如何将焦点从“行淫的动作”转移到“看见、动念并在心里犯奸淫”的过程。

The Greek term βλέπω (blepo) simply means ‘to see, to notice, to glance at" someone (LN 1996, 277 §24.7).
希腊语词汇 βλέπω（blepo）仅仅指“看见、注意到、瞥见”某人（LN 1996, 277 §24.7）。

However the phrase "everyone who is looking" (pas ho blepon), suggests a deliberate, sustained, or cultivating gaze rather than a merely incidental glance (Hagner 1993, 135; Davies and Allison 1988, 527).
然而，“凡看见”（pas ho blepon）这一表达指的是一种蓄意的、持续的或滋养邪念的凝视，而非偶然的瞥见（Hagner 1993, 135; Davies and Allison 1988, 527）。

Such a purposeful look with the purpose "of lusting" (pros to epithymesai auten) turns into adultery in the heart.[^5]
这种带着“动淫念”目的（pros to epithymesai auten）的蓄意凝视，在心里就演变成了奸淫。[^5]

We live in a world where it’s challenging to go through even a single day without being bombarded with images or media that are intentionally crafted to stimulate us sexually, or create sexual longing or desire.
我们生活在一个艰难的世界里，甚至一天之中都不免遭到故意精心制作的图像或媒体信息的轰炸，旨在刺激我们的性欲或制造性的渴望。

Yet Jesus is not here addressing such accidental attraction, but rather when an incidental look turns into a purposeful and settled gaze - longing holds potential to become adultery in the heart.
然而，耶稣在这里指的并不是这种偶然的吸引，而是当偶然的注视演变成有目的、沉溺的凝视时——这种渴望就具备了在心里演变为奸淫的可能。

Jesus therefore exposes the covetous desires that lie beneath adultery, and demonstrates that sin begins in the heart before it manifests itself in outward action (Davies and Allison 1988, 526-28; Carson 2010, 146-47).
因此，耶稣揭露了隐藏在奸淫背后的贪婪欲望，并表明罪在表现为外在行为之前，早已始于内心（Davies and Allison 1988, 526-28; Carson 2010, 146-47）。

Being a disciple of Christ, and seeking God's kingdom and His righteousness, therefore is not merely about “behavioral management” or external obedience - instead it is about being transformed from the inside out - from within the heart.
因此，作基督的门徒、寻求神的国和祂的义，绝不仅是关于“行为管理”或外在的顺服——而是关于从内到外、从心开始的生命翻转。

##### c. The Example of David and Bathsheba (2 Sam 11:2-4) 大卫与拔示巴的鉴戒（撒下 11:2-4）

Bible commentator R. T. France (2007, 204 § Matt 5:27-30) notes that the Greek Septuagint (LXX) uses the same word ἐπιθυμέω (epithymeo - lust) in 2 Sam 11:2-4 when David looked with lust at Bathsheba, when he saw her bathing on her roof.
圣经注释家 R. T. France（2007, 204 § Matt 5:27-30）指出，旧约希腊文译本（七十士译本）在撒母耳记下11:2-4中使用相同的词 ἐπιθυμέω（epithymeo，动淫念/贪恋），描述大卫在房顶上看见拔示巴沐浴并对她动了淫念。

To bathe on the roof of a house in the coolness of the evening was a common practice at that time, and culturally David ought to have given her privacy, especially in view of Bathsheba taking a ritual bath of cleansing after her monthly period (2 Sam 11:2-5).
傍晚在房顶上乘凉沐浴是当时普遍的习俗，从文化习惯来看，大卫本应尊重她的隐私，特别是注意到拔示巴当时是在月经洁净后行律法上的洁净礼（撒下 11:2-5）。

It's very sobering to see how David, even as a man of God, transfixed by what he saw, cast off all restraint, and ignored the warning of his servant that Bathsheba was the wife of Uriah.
令人警醒的是，大卫即使作为神所设立的人，竟被眼所见的深深吸引，抛开了一切克制，甚至无视仆人的提醒——拔示巴乃是乌利亚的妻子。

Yet with the hook of lust firmly swallowed, David ordered to get her, so that he could get what he wanted.
然而在吞下情欲的钩子后，大卫下令将她召来，以满足自己的私欲。

Any sympathy we might have for David for what initially seemed like an accidental glance quickly dissipates as we witness how he exploits his position of power, influence, and wealth to satisfy his desires and then conceal them.
我们最初可能对大卫抱有同情，以为那不过是无意中的一瞥，但当我们目睹他如何利用自己的权势、影响力和财富来满足私欲并试图掩盖时，那份同情便荡然无存。

From a woman's perspective you have to see the horror and injustice of turning someone created in the image of God into merely an object for satisfaction.
从女性的角度来看，你必须看到将一个按神形象所造的人降格为满足私欲的工具，是多么恐怖与不公。

At the same time you wonder whether Bathsheba could have been more discreet in protecting her privacy and not creating a stumbling block for those who might happen to gaze upon her.
同时你也会想，拔示巴是否本可以更加谨慎地保护自己的隐私，免得给偶然看见她的人造成跌倒的破口。

Scripture gives us no indication that Bathsheba resisted or protested the King's advances, which leaves us to wonder if she had been a willing participant in the adultery?
圣经并没有记载拔示巴反抗或抗议国王的追求，这不禁让人思考她是否也在其中默许并参与了这起奸淫？

We could further speculate that her husband’s prolonged absence may have made her both emotionally and physically vulnerable to the attention and advances of a man who made her feel special, beautiful, or desirable.
我们可以进一步推测，她丈夫长期的离家征战，可能使她在情感和身体上都变得脆弱，面对一个让她感到被重视、美丽或有吸引力的男人时，难以抵挡其关怀与追求。

The fact that he was a man of means and possibilities may have caused her to cast off her restraints as well.
而大卫拥有的地位与财富，也可能使她放下了原本的戒备。

The point I am making is that whether due to lust that enters through the eyes of a man, or whether caused by loneliness, emotional neglect, or romantic longing - both men and women are vulnerable to this sin of adultery that might strike at your heart unexpectedly.
我想表达的是，无论是通过男性眼睛进入的情欲，还是因孤单、情感疏离或浪漫渴望所引发的念头——男女都极易受到这种奸淫之罪的侵害，它会在意想不到的时候突袭你的内心。

Jesus was frustrated with the Pharisees' hypocritical handling of the adultery law, because the OT provided ample teaching against such lustful longing.
耶稣对法利赛人假冒伪善地处理奸淫律法感到愤慨，因为旧约圣经对这种贪婪的情欲早已给出了丰富的教导。

To begin with, the Tenth Commandment very clearly deals with this issue of covetousness when it says "You shall not covet your neighbor’s house. You shall not covet your neighbor’s wife" (Exod 20:17).
首先，第十条诫命非常明确地处理了贪婪的问题，经上说：“不可贪恋人的房屋；也不可贪恋人的妻子”（出埃及记 20:17）。

An Israelite woman certainly would have understood the law as "you shall not covet your neighbor's husband".
以色列女性当然也会明白这条律法的涵义包括“不可贪恋邻舍的丈夫”。

The path to covetousness for a woman might look different than for a man - perhaps longing for connection, intimacy, being listened to, prospects of a good income and home etc... Yet it still enters the heart through what you see or hear, and is still covetous lust.
女性走向贪婪的路径可能与男性不同——或许是渴望关注、亲密感、被倾听，或是对优渥生活和家庭的憧憬等……但它依然是通过眼见或耳闻进入内心，依然属于贪婪的情欲。

The OT prophet Job is a good example for us as men. He remained vigilant by saying "I made a covenant with my eyes not to look lustfully at a girl" (Job 31:1 NIV).
旧约中的约伯为男性树立了极佳的榜样。他保持警惕说道：“我与眼睛立约，怎能凝视处女呢？”（约伯记 31:1）

Solomon, perhaps with his father David's sin in mind, urged his son not to lust in his heart after the beauty of the immoral woman, nor to be captivated by her eyes or by her smooth tongue (Prov 6:24-25).
所罗门或许牢记父亲大卫的教训，劝诫儿子心中不要恋慕恶妇的美色，也不要被她眼皮勾引或谄媚的舌头所迷住（箴言 6:24-25）。

##### d. Jesus convicts us all as sexual sinners (v. 28) 耶稣定罪我们所有人都是性罪人（第28节）

Have a look again at v. 28:
让我们再次细读第28节：

> 28 But I tell you that anyone who looks at a woman lustfully **has already** committed adultery with her in his heart.
> 28只是我告诉你们，凡看见妇女就动淫念的，这人心里**已经**与她犯奸淫了。

Notice the sense of surprise and shock here to discover that you may have already committed adultery in your heart...(v. 28).
请注意，当发现自己可能在心里“已经犯了奸淫”时，那种惊恐与震惊……（第28节）。

When Jesus speaks of the heart (карδία, kardia), He is figuratively referring to our inner-life with all its secret thoughts, feelings, motives and desires that only you and God can see (LN 1996, 320 § 26.3).
当耶稣谈到“心”（карδία, kardia）时，祂比喻性地指我们的内心世界，包含只有你和神能看见的所有隐秘思想、情感、动机和欲望（LN 1996, 320 § 26.3）。

Jesus' words bring home a shocking realization, that we are all sexual sinners, that we have, and continue to sin with lustful eyes and covetous hearts.
耶稣的话带给我们震撼的警醒：我们都是性罪人，我们曾经并仍在借着眼中的情欲和贪婪的心犯罪。

So Jesus here is not merely convicting the Pharisees, but He convicts all of us as sexual sinners.
因此，耶稣在这里不仅是在定罪法利赛人，祂更是在揭露我们所有人作为性罪人的本相。

Jon Courson notes...
章伯斯/康森（Jon Courson）在此指出：

> “We have all looked with lust in our heart, thus we’re all adulterers. Truly, there is none righteous, no not one” (Courson 2003, 29 § Matt 5:27).
> “我们都在心里动过淫念，因此我们都是犯奸淫的人。正如经上所记，没有义人，连一个也没有。”（Courson 2003, 29 § Matt 5:27）

Jesus’ purpose in Matthew 5 was to use the Law of God to reveal, and indeed to convict all of us who are convinced of our own righteousness, that our hearts are desperately wicked and in need of God’s work of grace and restoration.
耶稣在马太福音5章的意图，是借着神的律法来显明并定罪我们这些自以为义的人，显明我们的心比万物都诡诈，迫切需要神恩典与医治的彰显。

#### 2) Sin in the heart demands decisive repentance  内心的罪需要果断的悔改（5:29-30）

<img src="https://1drv.ms/i/c/a28b460cc0654099/IQQR4cFPVl0dR5_b9Z4pzTOwAU0O160R-LNATq-GRHEutsI?width=660" width="660" height="auto" />

As we move to vv. 29-30, notice how Jesus underlines the seriousness of this sin and the importance of timely and decisive action.
当我们转到第29-30节时，请注意耶稣如何强调这种罪的严重性，以及采取及时果断行动的必要性。

> 29 If your right eye causes you to sin, gouge it out and throw it away. It is better for you to lose one part of your body than for your whole body to be thrown into hell. 30 And if your right hand causes you to sin, cut it off and throw it away. It is better for you to lose one part of your body than for your whole body to go into hell.
> 29若是你的右眼叫你跌倒，就剜出来丢掉，宁可失去百体中的一体，不叫全身丢在地狱里。 30若是右手叫你跌倒，就砍下来丢掉，宁可失去百体中的一体，不叫全身下入地狱。」

<img src="https://1drv.ms/i/c/a28b460cc0654099/IQSweelzOJpTQp1jhTRZz4d2Abx1akvzbBm8z02-agndwIs?width=660" width="660" height="auto" />

It goes without saying that Jesus doesn’t actually want us to physically gouge out our eye for lust or cut off our hand for touching what’s forbidden.[^6]
不言而喻，耶稣并不是真的要我们在身体上剜出眼睛或砍下右手。[^6]

His point is that our covetous and lustful desires are so serious that it's worth the considerable cost of stopping them both at the source and in their outflow into action.[^7]
祂的意思是，我们贪婪与情欲的罪是如此严重，以至于值得付出一切代价在源头和行动流出点上将其斩断。[^7]

##### a. The self-reinforcing cycle of sin - explained (vv. 29-30)罪自我强化循环的解析（第29-30节）

What is particularly striking is that Jesus does not simply command us to stop lusting in our heart.
特别引人注意的是，耶稣并没有简单地吩咐我们“停止在心里动淫念”。

Instead, He specifically calls us to deal decisively with both the eye and the hand.
相反，祂特别呼召我们要果断地处理“眼睛”和“手”。

> ..If your right eye causes you to stumble, gouge it out and throw it away..(v. 29)
> “……若是你的右眼叫你跌倒，就剜出来丢掉……”（第29节）
> 
> ..And if your right hand causes you to stumble, cut it off and throw it away...(v. 30)
> “……若是右手叫你跌倒，就砍下来丢掉……”（第30节）

He addresses both the inflow into the heart and the outflow from the heart.
祂同时处理了进心的源头（眼睛）和出心的流向（手）。

Why? A self-reinforcing cycle of sin can develop and continue for months or even years unless it is decisively broken. Notice the pattern in the diagram.
为什么？因为罪的自我强化循环一旦形成，就会持续数月甚至数年，除非被果断斩断。请看图表中的模式：

<img src="https://1drv.ms/i/c/a28b460cc0654099/IQQWnFWWZivYQre-Ok7jox3hAcr5cIMBxIQV4q0BIML4ilI?width=660" width="660" height="auto" />

The eye feeds the heart, and the heart directs the eye toward what it desires.
眼睛滋养内心，而内心又指引眼睛去看它所渴望的事物。

Those desires then move the hand to act, bringing a moment of satisfaction.
这些欲望随后驱使手去行动，带来暂时的满足。

Yet that satisfaction does not extinguish the desire; it strengthens it.
然而，这种暂时的满足并没有熄灭欲望，反而加强了欲望。

The stronger desire seeks further stimulation through the eye, and the cycle begins again with increasing intensity.
更强烈的欲望进一步通过眼睛寻求刺激，循环再次开始，强度不断递增。

This helps explain why Jesus speaks so forcefully about the eye and the hand.
这解释了为什么耶稣对眼睛和手讲得如此斩钉截铁。

He is teaching us as His disciples to take radical action against anything that either fuels sinful desires at their source or enables their expression in practice.
祂在教导我们作为祂的门徒，要采取果断激进的行动，斩断任何在源头滋养罪恶欲望，或在实践中促成罪恶表达的事物。

##### b. The self-reinforcing cycle of sin - illustrated 罪自我强化循环的比喻

To illustrate, imagine a man entering midlife who has long dreamed of owning a powerful and expensive Harley Davidson motorcycle.
为了说明这一点，想象一位步入中年的男士（就像我自己），长期梦想拥有一辆马力强劲且昂贵的哈雷摩托车。

At first, the desire is relatively small. He begins watching videos, reading reviews, and browsing photos online. The more attention he gives it, the more the desire grows.
起初，这种欲望相对微小。他开始在网上看视频、读测评、浏览照片。他给予的关注越多，欲望就越发膨胀。

Before long, he visits a dealership, sits on the motorcycle, and perhaps takes it for a test ride.
不久，他来到专卖店，跨坐在摩托车上，甚至进行试驾。

These experiences bring a measure of satisfaction, but they also deepen his longing.
这些体验带给他暂时的满足，却也加深了他的渴望。

Gradually, his thoughts, conversations, time, and money begin to revolve around acquiring what his heart desires.
渐渐地，他的思想、谈吐、时间和金钱开始全部围绕着获取他内心所渴望的东西运转。

If he wants to break the hold that this desire has on his heart and mind, he must cut off both the sources that feed it and the experiences that continually reinforce it.
如果他想斩断这种欲望对他内心和思想的掌控，他就必须斩断滋养它的源头以及不断强化它的体验。

##### c. The self-reinforcing cycle of sin - applied 罪自我强化循环的应用

Imagine you reconnect with an old friend or former boyfriend/girlfriend on social media.
想象一下，你在社交媒体上与一位老朋友或前男友/前女友重新取得了联系。

At first, it seems completely innocent. You look at a few photos, read some updates, and wonder how life has turned out for them. But the more attention you give them, the more often they occupy your thoughts.
起初，这似乎完全无辜。你看了几张照片，读了些动态，好奇他们近况如何。但你给的关注越多，他们占据你思想的频率就越高。

Before long, you find yourself checking their profile regularly, looking forward to new posts or messages, and imagining what life might have been like if things had turned out differently.
不久，你发现自己定期查看他们的主页，期待新动态或消息，并幻想如果当年有不同的选择，生活会是什么样子。

These interactions bring a measure of satisfaction, but they also deepen the emotional attachment.
这些互动带给你某种程度的满足，但同时也加深了情感上的依恋。

Gradually, your thoughts, emotions, and attention become increasingly focused on that person.
渐渐地，你的思想、情感和注意力越来越集中在那个人身上。

What began as curiosity has grown into something far more powerful - longing.
始于好奇的试探，如今已演变成一股强大得多的渴望。

This is why Jesus calls for radical action. Sin must be confronted both at its source and at its point of expression.
这正是为什么耶稣呼召我们采取果断行动。罪必须在其源头和表达点上同时被正面击退。

**Whatever feeds sinful desire and whatever gives it opportunity to grow must be dealt with decisively.[^8]**
**凡是滋养罪恶欲望的事物，凡是给罪恶提供生根发芽机会的破口，都必须果断处理。[^8]**

> "The threat of lust is so strong and its dangers so great that a price comparable to the removal of eye or hand is not too great to pay as one seeks freedom from it..." (Thomas 1998, §4624; Hagner 1993, 120; Stagg 1969, 109).
> “情欲的威吓是如此强大，其危险是如此巨大，以至于当一个人寻求从中得自由时，付出与剜眼砍手相当的代价都不算过分……”（Thomas 1998, §4624; Hagner 1993, 120; Stagg 1969, 109）

This might mean that you are reminded this morning that there might be a person that you are giving too much thought to, or allowing secret longing for.
这可能意味着今天早上提醒你，可能正有一个人占据了你过多的思想，或者你正对其存有隐秘的渴望。

##### Sin begins in the heart, not merely in the act (Matt 5:27–28) 罪始于内心，而不仅仅在于行为本身（太 5:27–28）

The religious leaders conveniently restricted the Seventh Commandment ("Do not commit adultery") to physical acts of illicit intercourse.
宗教领袖投机取巧地将第七条诫命（“不可奸淫”）局限于非法性行为的肢体动作。

Jesus rejects this narrow definition, establishing that kingdom righteousness requires transformed motives and desires rather than superficial behavior management.
耶稣拒绝了这种狭隘的定义，确立了天国的义需要翻转的动机与渴望，而非表面的行为管理。

Jesus does not condemn us for the accidental glance or natural visual attraction, but warns that a deliberate, sustained gaze cultivated for the explicit purpose of self-gratifying lust is already adultery.
耶稣并没有因偶然的瞥见或正常的视觉吸引而定罪我们，但祂警示说，为满足自私情欲而故意滋养的沉溺注视，在心里就已经演变成了奸淫。

Such adultery of the heart is rooted in coveting what belongs to someone else.
这种内心的奸淫深深根植于贪恋属于他人的事物。

Lust objectifies an image-bearer created to be loved and honored, reducing them to an object to be consumed.
情欲将按神形象所造、本应受到爱护与尊重的形象承担者物化，将其降格为可供消费的工具。

##### Sin in the heart demands decisive repentance (Matt 5:29–30) 内心的罪需要果断的悔改（太 5:29–30）

Jesus uses shocking hyperbole—gouging out an eye or cutting off a hand—not to command physical self-mutilation, but to demand radical action against whatever traps or ensnares us (skandalizo).
耶稣使用了令人震惊夸张手法——剜出眼睛或砍下手——并非命令我们残害肉体，而是要求我们对任何陷害或网罗我们的试探（skandalizo）采取果断决绝的行动。

By addressing both the eye (what enters the heart) and the hand (what proceeds from the heart), Jesus calls us as His disciples to eliminate the environments, habits, media, and relationships that feed wrongful desires before they cause spiritual destruction.
借着处理眼睛（进入内心的源头）和手（从内心流出的行动），耶稣呼召我们作为祂的门徒，在邪情私欲引发属灵毁灭之前，斩断那些滋养不良欲望的环境、习惯、媒体和人际关系。

##### What then is the path of hope and restoration in the Sermon on the Mount?  在《登山宝训》中，何为盼望与医治之路？

<img src="https://1drv.ms/i/c/a28b460cc0654099/IQRQNIfBPSVcTroAN4THFnMVAd-WtXEZBaRtX23_CANxFpY?width=660" width="660" height="auto" />

Whether our struggle is lust, covetousness, or some other misplaced desire, none of us can stand before these words and claim innocence.
无论我们的挣扎是情欲、贪婪，还是其他错位的欲望，我们中没有一个人能在这些话语面前自称无辜。

Jesus exposes the hidden motives of our hearts not to leave us crushed under moralistic condemnation or hopeless self-effort.
耶稣揭露我们内心的隐秘动机，绝不是为了让我们在道德主义的定罪或绝望的自我挣扎中溃败。

Instead there is much hope even within the Sermon on the Mount.
相反，即使在《登山宝训》中，也包含着无比丰富的盼望。

Let's return to Jesus' opening words in the Beatitudes:
让我们重温耶稣在八福中的开篇宣言：

> “Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven. Blessed are those who mourn, for they will be comforted... Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they will be filled.” (Matt. 5:3-4, 6)
> “虚心的人有福了！因为天国是他们的。 哀恸的人有福了！因为他们必得安慰。……饥渴慕义的人有福了！因为他们必得饱足。”（太 5:3-4, 6）

Jesus' teaching this morning has exposed the sin in every one of our hearts, yet He does not expose our sin in order to drive us away from Him. He exposes our sin in order to draw us to Him.
今天早上耶稣的教导揭露了我们每个人心中的罪，但祂显露我们的罪绝非要将我们赶走，而是要将我们吸引到祂面前。

From the very beginning of the Sermon on the Mount, He invites us to come as we are - poor in spirit, mourning over our sin, and hungry and thirsty for His righteousness - the righteousness that we do not possess in ourselves.
从《登山宝训》的开头，祂就邀请我们带着真实的本相而来——承认灵里贫穷，为自己的罪哀恸，并饥渴慕义——慕那我们在自己里面绝不拥有的义。

And to those of us who come to Him with repentant faith, Jesus makes wonderful promises - the kingdom will belong to us, we will be comforted, and we will indeed be filled and clothed with Christ's righteousness.
对于我们这些带着悔改的信心来到祂面前的人，耶稣许下了美妙的应许——天国将属于我们，我们必得安慰，并且我们必得饱足，披戴基督的义。

Therefore, our hope is not found in our ability to conquer every sinful desire by our own strength.
因此，我们的盼望不在于靠自己的力量去征服每一个罪恶的欲望。

Our hope is found in Christ, who forgives sinners, who comforts the broken-hearted, and who gives us His righteousness through faith in Him.
我们的盼望在于基督——祂赦免罪人，安慰伤心的人，并藉着对祂的信心将祂的义赐给我们。

So if Jesus' words have convicted you this morning, do not run from Him. Run to Him.
所以，如果今天早上耶稣的话触动并定罪了你，不要逃离祂，要奔向祂。

Confess your sin, seek His mercy, and trust His grace.
承认你的罪，寻求祂的怜悯，仰望祂的恩典。

The One who exposes the sin of the heart is also the One who heals and transforms the heart.
那位揭露人心之罪的主，正是医治并翻转人心的主。

And as we look to the closing words of the Sermon on the Mount, we hear Jesus say:
当我们看到《登山宝训》的结语时，我们听见耶稣说：

> "Everyone who hears these words of mine and puts them into practice is like a wise man who built his house on the rock" (Matt. 7:24).
> “所以，凡听见我这话就去行的，好比一个聪明人，把房子盖在磐石上。”（太 7:24）

Instead of fixing our eyes on what we cannot have, Jesus calls us to fix our eyes on Him.
耶稣呼召我们，不要注视那些我们不该拥有的事物，而是要把眼目专注于祂。

Instead of allowing our hearts to be shaped by sinful desires, He calls us to build our lives upon His Word.
祂呼召我们，不要让心被罪恶的欲望塑造，而是要把生命建造在祂的话语之上。

As we come to Christ in faith and humility, listening to His voice, feeding on His truth, and putting His teaching into practice, the Holy Spirit gradually transforms our hearts and minds.
当我们带着信心与谦卑来到基督面前，倾听祂的声音，以祂的真理为粮，并将祂的教导付诸实践时，圣灵就会渐渐翻转我们的心意更新。

Old desires begin to lose their grip, and new desires begin to take their place. Our love for Christ grows stronger than our love for sin.
旧的欲望开始失去掌控，新的渴望开始取而代之。我们对基督的爱将胜过对罪恶的爱。

This is the hope of the Sermon on the Mount.
这正是《登山宝训》带来的盼望。

Jesus does not merely expose the sin within us; He also provides the foundation upon which we can build a new life.
耶稣不仅揭露我们里面的罪，祂更为我们奠定了建造崭新生命的磐石根基。

The One who reveals the sickness of the heart is also the One who heals it.
那位显明人心病态的主，正是医治人心的救主。

And as we build our lives upon Him, He graciously transforms us into the very people He calls us to be. Amen.
当我们把生命建造在祂之上时，祂必按着恩典将我们塑造成为祂所呼召我们成为的人。阿们。

### Closing Prayer 结束祷告

**Heavenly Father,**
天父，

We come before You as those whose hearts have been exposed by Your Word.
我们来到祢面前，因着祢的话语，我们的心已被赤露敞开。

You see what no one else can see: our thoughts, desires, motives, and secret longings.
祢看见无人能看见的景况：我们的思想、欲望、动机和隐秘的渴望。

We confess that we have sinned, and fixed our eyes on things that do not belong to us and allowed our hearts to covet or lust.
我们向祢承认我们犯了罪，将眼目定睛于不属于我们的事物，容让心生出贪恋与情欲。

As those who are poor in spirit, we mourn over our sin and ask for Your mercy.
作为虚心的人，我们为自己的罪哀恸，求祢施恩怜悯。

Create in us clean hearts, and renew us from within.
求祢在境内为我们造清洁的心，使我们里面重新有正直的灵。

**Lord Jesus Christ,**
主耶稣基督，

Thank You that You did not come to condemn us as sinners but to save us.
感谢祢，祢来不是要定罪我们这些罪人，乃是要拯救我们。

Thank You that You bore our guilt upon the cross and gave us Your righteousness through faith.
感谢祢，祢在十字架上担当了我们的罪尤，并因着信将祢的义赐给了我们。

As we are convicted by Your words, help us not to run from You, but to run to You.
当我们被祢的话语责备时，求祢帮助我们不要逃离祢，而是奔向祢。

Teach us to build our lives upon the rock of Your teaching, trusting that Your grace is sufficient for us and that Your forgiveness is greater than our failures.
求祢教导我们将生命建造在祢教导的磐石上，确信祢的恩典够我们用，祢的赦免胜过我们所有的失败。

**Holy Spirit,**
圣灵啊，

Transform us from the inside out. Break the power of sinful desires and replace them with a deeper love for Christ.
求祢从内到外翻转我们。打破罪恶欲望的捆绑，以对基督更深的爱取而代之。

Give us wisdom to recognize whatever feeds sin in our lives and courage to deal with it decisively.
赐给我们智慧，去辨识生命中任何滋养罪恶破口的事物；赐给我们勇气，去果断地切断它们。

As we listen to Your voice, feed on Your Word, and walk in obedience, shape our thoughts, desires, and affections so that our love for Christ grows stronger than our love for sin.
当我们倾听祢的声音，以祢的话语为食，并在顺服中同行时，求祢塑造我们的心思、欲望和情感，使我们对基督的爱胜过对罪恶的爱。

Make us a people who are pure in heart and who delight in Christ's Word.
使我们成为清心的人，成为喜悦基督话语的人。

**For Yours is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory forever and ever. Amen.**
因为国度、权柄、荣耀，全是祢的，直到永远。阿们。
