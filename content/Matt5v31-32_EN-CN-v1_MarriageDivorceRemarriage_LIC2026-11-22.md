### 2. 摩西实际许可的规定 / What Moses actually allowed (Deut 24:1-4)

###### a. 摩西仅在性不贞的情况下许可离婚 / Moses permitted divorce in cases of sexual infidelity

![[Pasted image 20261122090133.png]]

Moses acknowledged the reality and destructive nature of marital breakdown, but he only allowed it only for cases of sexual infidelity, not for any and every reason.
摩西承认婚姻破裂的现实和破坏性，但他只允许在性不贞的情况下离婚，而不是出于任何和所有原因。

A careful reading the Hebrew text here clearly shows that the displeasure of the husband, is connected only with an sexual indecency, and not a displeasure for any or every reason.
仔细阅读这里的希伯来文文本清楚地表明，丈夫的不快仅与性方面的不洁相连，而不是出于任何或所有原因的不快。

The term “indecency” translates  the Hebrew word עֶרְוָה‎ (ěr·wāh), which literally means the shameful exposing of her nakedness.
“不洁”一词翻译自希伯来文 עֶרְוָה‎ (ěr·wāh)，字面意思是可耻地袒露她的下体。

This word is an euphemism for illicit sex (Swanson 1997, 6872).
这个词是违法性行为的委婉说法（Swanson 1997, 6872）。

Joseph found himself in this predicament when he discovered Mary’s pregnancy, and consequently resolved to quietly divorce her (Matthew 1:18-25).

In asking, “Haven’t you read?” (19:4), Jesus questions how you can shape your thinking on marriage and divorce only on the one permissive law, while ignoring the broad scope of God's design and purpose of marriage.
在问“你们没有念过吗？”（19:4）时，耶稣质问人们怎么能仅仅根据一条许可性的律法来塑造对婚姻和离婚的看法，却忽视了神对婚姻设计与旨意的宏大蓝图。

Jesus therefore challenges liberal thinking that undermines the distinctions between men and women,  and any view that undermines that sanctity of marriage in God's sight.
因此，耶稣挑战了削弱男女区别的自由主义思想，以及任何在神眼中破坏婚姻神圣性的观点。

##### c. 神建立了一夫一妻的婚姻 / God establishes a marriage between an man and a women


God’s creation purpose was not just to create men and women, but also to unite a man and a woman in marriage.
神的创造旨意不仅仅是创造男女，也是为了在婚姻中将一男一女连合。

God saw that it was not good for Adam to be alone, and provided for him companion and a suitable helper (Gen 2:18).
神见亚当独居不好，就为他预备了一位伴侣和合适的帮手（创世记 2:18）。

Notice how a marriage is established in God's sight by means of a process of leaving, cleaving and becoming one (Matt 19:5, cf Gen 2:24).
请注意婚姻在神眼中是如何通过离开、连合与合一的过程建立起来的（马太福音 19:5，参照创世记 2:24）。

###### Read Matt 19:5 / 宣读经文：马太福音 19:5

> and (God) said, ‘For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife, and the two will become one flesh’?
5 并且说：『因此，人要离开父母，与妻子连合，二人成为一体。』这经你们没有念过吗？」

Observe the process of “leaving, cleaving, and becoming one flesh” by which God establishes a legitimate marriage between a man and a woman.
观察神建立一男一女合法婚姻的“离开、连合、二人成为一体”的过程。

![[Pasted image 20261122090546.png]]

There are three processes that is essential for a marriage to be established.
建立婚姻有三个不可或缺的过程：

###### 你必须“离开” / You must leave

Firstly,  both the man and the women must leave the house of the parents,  and come out from under the dependency and authority the father and mother.
首先，男人和女人都必须离开父母的家，脱离对父母的依赖和管辖。

A failure to leave their parents, whether physically, emotionally, or financially, undermines the successful establishment of the marriage and creates numerous problems in the relationship.
未能离开父母——无论是在身体上、情感上还是经济上——都会损害婚姻的成功建立，并在关系中引发许多问题。

###### 你必须“连合” / You must cleave

Secondly,  there needs to be joined or glued together in a lifelong, loving, faithful and exclusive relationship, while renouncing all former allegiances.
其次，必须在终身、充满爱、忠贞和排他的关系中联合或紧紧胶合在一起，同时放弃过去所有的依附。

This suggests an event such as the wedding ceremony with marriage vows before God,  the law and witness in which you promise life long love, faithfulness and mutual care.
这意味着诸如婚礼之类的仪式，在神、法律和见证人面前立下婚姻誓言，承诺终身相爱、忠贞和相互关怀。

###### 你必须“成为一体” / You must become one

Thirdly, God designed marriage to be sealed by a unique emotional, covenantal, and physical union between one man and one woman.
第三，神设计婚姻是由一男一女之间独特的性情、盟约和身体合一来印证的。

##### d. 婚外性行为阻碍神创造的旨意 / Extramarital Sex Hinders God's Creation Purpose

God designed marriage to be a lifelong covenant in which a husband and wife are united emotionally, spiritually, and physically.
神设计婚姻是一项终身的盟约，其中夫妻在情感、属灵和身体上合而为一。

Sexual intimacy was never meant to stand alone; it was intended to strengthen and seal that covenant bond.
性亲密从来不是孤立存在的；它的目的是巩固和印证那盟约的纽带。

**Premarital sex**  works against God's design because it takes the gift of sexual intimacy and separates it from the lifelong commitment that gives it permanence, security, and meaning.
**婚前性行为**违背了神的设计，因为它将性亲密的礼物与赋予其永久性、安全感和意义的终身承诺分离开来。

What God intended as a covenant-renewing act becomes disconnected from covenant itself.
神所设计作为更新盟约的行为，反而脱离了盟约本身。

**Cohabitation**  often falls short of God's purpose because it can reduce commitment to a private arrangement that lasts only as long as it remains convenient, rather than a public and lifelong promise of faithfulness before God and others.
**同居**往往达不到神的目的，因为它将承诺降格为私下的安排，只在方便时维持，而不是在神和众人面前公开且终身的忠贞许诺。

**Adultery**  strikes at the heart of God's design for marriage.
**奸淫**直击神婚姻设计的核心。

It violates the exclusive bond between husband and wife, shattering trust, undermining security, and wounding the intimacy that God intended to flourish within marriage.
它破坏了夫妻之间排他的纽带，粉碎了信任，损害了安全感，并伤害了神旨在使之在婚姻中蓬勃发展的亲密关系。

**Divorce**  likewise falls short of God's creational intent.
**离婚**同样达不到神创造的本意。

What God designed to be a lifelong union is torn apart, often leaving deep wounds for spouses, children, and entire families.
神所设计终身结合的关系被撕裂，往往给配偶、子女和整个家庭留下深深的创伤。

The very language of Scripture recognizes the painful reality of two lives being separated after having become "one flesh."
圣经本身的话语也承认了两个生命在“成为一体”之后被撕裂分离的痛苦现实。

One of the lesser-discussed tragedies of premarital sexual relationships and repeated cohabiting relationships is that destroys a person's ability to "cleave" wholeheartedly to a future spouse.
婚前性关系和反复同居关系中鲜被讨论的悲剧之一，是它破坏了一个人向未来的配偶全心全意“连合”的能力。

Every intimate relationship leaves an imprint. Every breakup carries the potential for emotional wounds, guardedness, mistrust, and relational patterns that are brought into future marriages.
每一段亲密关系都会留下印记。每一次分手都带有情感创伤、戒备、不信任以及带入未来婚姻的关系模式的风险。

**Young people** , this is why purity is not merely about following rules.
**年轻人**，这就是为什么圣洁不仅关乎遵守规则。

It is about protecting a future that aligns with the blessing of God's good purpose for your future marriage.
它关乎保护一个符合神对你未来婚姻美好旨意与祝福的未来。

It is about preserving your capacity to give yourself fully and faithfully to the husband or wife God may one day give you.
它关乎保留你的能力，使你能将自己完全且忠贞地奉献给神有一天赐给你的丈夫或妻子。

By guarding yourself emotionally, spiritually, and physically today, you are preparing yourself to cleave deeply, joyfully, and wholeheartedly to your future marriage partner.
今天在情感、属灵和身体上保守自己，就是预备自己深切、喜乐和全心全意地与你未来的婚姻伴侣连合。

### 4. 耶稣宣告的圣洁标准 / What Jesus said (Matt 5:32, 19:9, Luke)

###### a. 耶稣确认性淫乱（Porneia）为合法离婚依据 / Jesus Upholds Sexual Immorality (Porneia) as a Legitimate Ground for Divorce (5:32; 19:9; cf. Luke 16:18)

Jesus upholds the teaching of Moses by recognizing sexual immorality as a legitimate ground for divorce. While divorce is permitted in such circumstances, it is never commanded.
耶稣承认性淫乱是离婚的合法依据，从而坚持了摩西的教导。虽然在这些情况下允许离婚，但绝不意味着吩咐离婚。

"But I tell you that anyone who divorces his wife, except for marital unfaithfulness, causes her to become an adulteress..." (Matt. 5:32)
「只是我告诉你们，凡休妻的，若不是为淫乱的缘故，就是叫她作淫妇了……」（马太福音 5:32）

![[Pasted image 20261122090819.png]]

The word translated "marital unfaithfulness" is  *porneia* , a broad term referring to sexual immorality.
翻译为“淫乱”的词是 porneia，这是一个指代性淫乱的广义词。

![[Pasted image 20261122090829.png]]

In the context of marriage, it includes adultery and other serious forms of sexual sin that violate the marriage covenant.
在婚姻的语境中，它包括奸淫和其他严重破坏婚姻盟约的性罪行。

At the same time, we must be careful not to encourage divorce too quickly where adultery or sexual unfaithfulness has occurred.
同时，在发生奸淫或性不贞时，我们必须小心不要过于匆忙地鼓励离婚。

A spouse who has suffered such betrayal will often need significant support, wisdom, and discernment from fellow believers and church leaders.
遭受此类背叛的配偶往往需要主内肢体和教会领袖的极大支持、智慧与分辨。

On the one hand, adultery is not an unforgivable sin. Christ died for adulterers as surely as He died for every other sinner. Through genuine repentance, He is able to forgive, restore, and heal what has been deeply broken.
一方面，奸淫并非不可赦免的罪。基督为犯奸淫的人舍命，正如他为其他每一个罪人舍命一样。通过真实的悔改，他能够饶恕、恢复和医治那被严重破坏的关系。

On the other hand, sexual unfaithfulness strikes at the very heart of marriage. By becoming one flesh with someone other than one's spouse, the adulterer commits a profound violation of the covenant that God established.
另一方面，性不贞直击婚姻的核心。通过与配偶以外的人成为一体，犯奸淫者极大地破坏了神所设立的盟约。

Therefore, wherever possible, every effort should be made to pursue repentance, forgiveness, and reconciliation.
因此，只要有可能，就应当尽一切努力去追求悔改、饶恕与和好。

Yet Jesus recognizes that persistent and unrepentant sexual immorality may so fundamentally rupture the marriage covenant that divorce becomes a legitimate option before God.
然而耶稣也承认，持续且不肯悔改的性淫乱可能会从根本上撕裂婚姻盟约，以至于离婚在神面前成为合法的选择。


##### b. 耶稣坚决反对任何和所有随意理由的离婚 / Jesus Rejects Divorce for Any and Every Reason (5:32; 19:9; cf. Luke 16:18)

**Read Matthew 5:31-32** / **宣读经文：马太福音 5:31-32**

>31**  “It has been said, ‘Anyone who divorces his wife must give her a certificate of divorce.’  32 But I tell you that anyone who divorces his wife, except for marital unfaithfulness, causes her to become an adulteress, and anyone who marries the divorced woman commits adultery.”
31 「又有话说：『人若休妻，就当给她休书。』 32 只是我告诉你们，凡休妻的，若不是为淫乱的缘故，就是叫她作淫妇了；人若娶这被休的妇人，也是犯奸淫了。」

Remember that Jesus is responding to the attitude and approach of the permissive culture of His day. He firmly rejects the practice of divorcing for any and every reason.
记住，耶稣是在回应他那个时代放任文化的态度和做法。他坚决拒绝因任何和所有理由而离婚的做法。

Jesus' points in verse 32 is that when a husband divorces his wife without legitimate grounds, he not only breaks covenant faithfulness himself, but he places her in a situation that can lead to adultery.
耶稣在第 32 节的要点是，当丈夫在没有合法依据的情况下休妻时，他不仅自己破坏了盟约的忠贞，而且将妻子置于可能导致奸淫的境地。

![[Pasted image 20261122090930.png]]
This may happen in several ways:
这可能会通过几种方式发生：

**(1)**  While the husband may have served her with a certificate of divorce, in the absence of legitimate grounds for divorce, the marriage union may still be valid in God's sight. A human document cannot necessarily dissolve what God has joined together.
**(1)** 虽然丈夫可能给她发了休书，但在缺乏合法离婚依据的情况下，婚姻联合在神眼中可能仍然有效。属人的文件并不一定能废除神所配合的。

**(2)**  By divorcing her and sending her away, he places her in circumstances where the marriage covenant is treated as though it has been dissolved, when in God's sight it may still remain intact.
**(2)** 通过休她并打发她走，他将她置于婚姻盟约被当作已被废除的环境中，而在神眼中，该盟约可能仍然完好无损。

**(3)**  As a result, whether through financial necessity, social vulnerability, or the longing for companionship, she may eventually enter into a relationship or marriage with another man. Yet if the original marriage still stands in God's sight, this would constitute adultery.
**(3)** 结果，无论是出于经济需要、社会脆弱性，还是对伴侣的渴望，她最终可能会与另一个男人建立关系或结婚。然而，如果原初的婚姻在神眼中仍然存在，这就构成了奸淫。

**(4)**  Likewise, if the original divorce lacked biblical grounds, the man who marries her also commits adultery, because in God's eyes the original marriage covenant still stands.
**(4)** 同样，如果最初的离婚缺乏圣经依据，娶她的男人也是犯奸淫了，因为在神眼中原初的婚姻盟约仍然存在。

Although Jesus presents this example from the perspective of a husband divorcing his wife, we know that in many societies today it is often the wife who initiates the divorce. The principle, however, remains the same.
虽然耶稣从丈夫休妻的角度提出了这个例子，但我们知道在今天的许多社会中，常常是妻子提出离婚。然而，原则是一样的。

**Read Mark 10:11-12****宣读经文：马可福音 10:11-12**

>**11**  He answered, “Anyone who divorces his wife and marries another woman commits adultery against her.
11 耶稣对他们说：「凡休妻另娶的，就是犯奸淫，辜负妻子；

>**12**  And if she divorces her husband and marries another man, she commits adultery.”
12 妻子若离弃丈夫另嫁，也是犯奸淫了。」

Jesus therefore applies the same standard to both husbands and wives. The issue is not whether the man or the woman initiates the divorce, but whether the marriage covenant is being broken contrary to God's will. God calls both husbands and wives to covenant faithfulness and rejects the practice of treating marriage as something that can be dissolved whenever it becomes difficult or inconvenient.
因此，耶稣对丈夫和妻子适用同样的标准。问题不在于男人还是女人提出离婚，而在于婚姻盟约是否违背神的旨意而被破坏。神呼召丈夫和妻子守约忠贞，并拒绝将婚姻视为只要艰难或不便就可以解除的做法。

## 总结与应用 / Conclusion and Summary

![[Pasted image 20261122085759.png]]


Dear friends as we conclude with this sobering teaching of Christ,  I would like to make a few practical applications from this passage.
亲爱的弟兄姐妹，当我们以基督这严肃的教导结束时，我想从这段经文中做出几点应用。

#### (1) 让我们停止在圣经中寻找漏洞来为我们的罪辩护 / Let us stop seeking loopholes in Scripture to justify our sin

![[Pasted image 20261122091024.png]]

Instead, let us bring our hardness of heart before God,  asking the Holy Spirit to enable us to respond with forgiveness and grace to our spouse.
相反，让我们将我们刚硬的心带到神面前，祈求圣灵使我们能够以饶恕和恩典回应配偶。

We should ask   *"What sin in our marriage needs to be repented of so we can stay together?"*  rather than _"How can I get out of it?
我们应该问：“在我们婚姻中有哪些罪需要悔改，好使我们能守在一起？”而不是问：“我怎样才能脱离这段婚姻？”

####  **2. Let us actively safeguard the creation pattern in our marriage:   Leave, Cleave, and Become One Flesh**/ 让我们积极维护婚姻中创世的模式：离开、连合、二人成为一体**

![[Pasted image 20261122091046.png]]

We can protect our marriages when we intentionally activate all three dimensions of leaving, cleaving and becoming one flesh: 
当我们有意识地激活离开、连合与合一这三个维度时，我们就能保护我们的婚姻：

1.  **Properly Leaving:**  We might need to establish appropriate bounderies from parents, or external dependencies so that our marriage and household may not be undermined. **合宜地离开：** 我们可能需要设立与父母或外在依赖的适当界限，使我们的婚姻和家庭不受损害。 

2.  **Faithfully Cleaving (**   **Kollao**   **):**  Let us repent from, and  guard our hearts against premarital or extramarital emotional attachments that impair deep relational bonding  **忠贞地连合（Kollao）：** 让我们痛悔并保守内心，抵御损害深层关系纽带的婚前或婚外情感依附

3.  **Nourishing One-Flesh Unity:**  Protecting both body and mind from objectifying lust (Matt 5:28) and treating one's spouse as an image-bearer of God to be cherished[1]. **滋养一体的合一：** 保护身体和心灵不受物化情欲的伤害（马太福音 5:28），并将配偶视为神所宝爱的形象承载者。

####  **3. Let us respond with truth and grace to those who experience the trauma of divorce** / 让我们以真理和恩典回应那些经历离婚创伤的人**

![[Pasted image 20261122091116.png]]
As a church we must not adopt a cold Pharisaical stance toward those who are going through or had experienced a divorce.  The heartace and trauma of divorce calls for a compassionate, caring and supportive respons to walk with fellow believers whose marriages may be in trouble or are breaking up.
作为教会，我们决不能对那些正在经历或曾经经历离婚的人采取冷酷的法利赛态度。离婚的心碎和创伤需要富有同情心、关怀和支持性的回应，与那些婚姻可能陷入困境或破裂的同工同道同行。

#### 4. Pray together as a family and a couple 夫妻与家庭一同祷告 / Pray together as a family and a couple

The saying, a family that prays together stays together seem to hold truth.  Academic research show that couples who practice their faith together - attending church together, serving together, and praying together experience more satisfying marriage relationships.
俗话说：“一起祷告的家庭终身同在”，这句话似乎确实有道理。学术研究表明，一起实践信仰的夫妻——一起参加教会、一起事奉、一起祷告——能经历更满意的婚姻关系。
