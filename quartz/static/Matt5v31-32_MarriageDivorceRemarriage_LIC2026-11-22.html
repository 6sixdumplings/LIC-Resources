Marriage Is Under Tremendous Pressure Worldwide 婚姻制度在全球范围内正面临巨大压力 Die Huwelik Is Wêreldwyd Onder Geweldige Druk

Since 2000, marriage rates across OECD nations have fallen by roughly 25% (from 5.1 to 3.8 per 1,000 people). First marriages are delayed into the late twenties and thirties, roughly 30% of births across the developed world occur outside marriage, and regional pressures in China and Thailand reveal deep fractures in cultural expectations of faithfulness.

自2000年以来，经合组织（OECD）国家的结婚率大幅下降了约25%（从千分之 5.1 降至千分之 3.8）。初婚年龄不断推迟至二十多岁末或三十多岁，发达国家约 30% 的新生儿出生在婚姻之外；而中国与泰国的婚姻数据，更折射出当代文化对婚姻忠贞认知的深刻动摇。

Sedert 2000 het huweliksyfers regoor OESO-lande met sowat 25% gedaal (van 5,1 tot 3,8 per 1 000 mense). Eerste huwelike word uitgestel tot laat twintigs en dertigs, sowat 30% van geboortes in ontwikkelde lande vind buite die huwelik plaas, en streeksdruk in China en Thailand onthul diep skeure in kulturele verwagtinge van trou.

Point 01 · Historical & Rabbinic Context 第一点 · 文士与拉比的历史背景 Punt 01 · Historiese & Rabbynse Konteks

What the Teachers Argued (Matt 5:31; 19:3; Deut 24:1) 教师与文士的争论（太 5:31；19:3；申 24:1） Wat die Leraars Redeneer Het (Matt 5:31; 19:3; Deut 24:1)

Jesus confronts a permissive first-century culture where divorce had become easy and routine. Historian Josephus notes that divorce was “purely a male prerogative, which required no legal hearing, merely the husband’s decision; Jewish law made no provision for a woman to initiate divorce” (Ant. 15.259). An entire Mishnah tractate (Giṭṭin) detailed how to write a valid dismissal certificate (apoluo).

耶稣在这里面对的是一个离婚极其容易且普遍的世代。犹太历史学家约瑟夫斯指出，当时“离婚纯粹是男性的特权，不需要经过法庭听证，只需丈夫单方面决定；犹太律法并未赋予妻子主动提出离婚的权利”（《犹太古史》15.259）。密西拿甚至有整部论著《休书篇》（Giṭṭin）专门规范如何书写合法的休书。

Jesus konfronteer ’n toegeeflike eersteeuse kultuur waar egskeiding maklik en alledaags geword het. Die historikus Josefus merk op dat egskeiding “suiwer ’n manlike voorreg was wat geen regsverhoor vereis het nie, bloot die man se besluit; Joodse wet het geen voorsiening gemaak vir ’n vrou om egskeiding te inisieer nie” (Ant. 15.259).

The Rabbinic Debate: Shammai vs. Hillel 拉比学派之争：沙迈学派 vs. 希勒尔学派 Die Rabbynse Debat: Sjammai teenoor Hillel

House of Shammai · Rigorist 沙迈学派 · 严谨派 Huis van Sjammai · Streng

Read 'erwat dabar (“something indecent” in Deut 24:1) as a unified phrase restricting divorce solely to serious sexual immorality or marital unfaithfulness.

将申命记 24:1 中的 'erwat dabar（“不合理的事 / 羞耻的事”）视为整体词组，主张唯有严重的性不忠才可离婚。

Het 'erwat dabar (“iets skandeliks” in Deut 24:1) gelees as ’n enkele begrip wat egskeiding uitsluitlik tot ernstige seksuele ontrou beperk.

House of Hillel · “Any Cause” Loophole 希勒尔学派 · “任何理由”漏洞 Huis van Hillel · “Enige Rede”-Skuiwergat

Split “if she finds no favor in his eyes” from “something indecent,” granting men liberty to divorce for any and every reason (burning dinner, personal dislike, or finding someone else)—so long as a paper certificate was handed over.

将“不蒙恩”与“有不合理的事”割裂解读，赋予丈夫“无论什么缘故”都可休妻的自由（哪怕做饭烧焦、心生厌烦或另有所爱），只要给一纸休书即可。

Het “as sy geen guns in sy oë vind nie” geskei van “iets skandeliks”, en mans die reg gegee om oor enige rede te skei (selfs gebrande kos of voorkeur), solank ’n skeibrief gegee is.

Point 02 · Deuteronomy 24:1–4 Case Law 第二点 · 申命记 24:1–4 的律法原意 Punt 02 · Deuteronomium 24:1–4 Gevalsreg

What the Law Actually Taught 摩西律法真正教导的是什么 Wat die Wet Werklik Geleër Het

The Pharisees treated Deuteronomy 24:1 as a command to divorce (“Why then did Moses command…?” Matt 19:7). Jesus corrected them: Moses permitted it as regulatory case law because of human hardness of heart (sklerokardia). Deuteronomy 24 does not endorse divorce; it regulates its messy aftermath to restrain abuse.

法利赛人把申命记 24:1 当作神吩咐人离婚的命令（“摩西为什么吩咐给妻子休书呢？”太 19:7）。耶稣纠正他们：摩西只是因为人的心硬（sklerokardia）而容许离婚，设立判例法来规范善后，绝非鼓励或赞成离婚。

Die Fariseërs het Deuteronomium 24:1 as ’n bevel tot egskeiding hanteer (“Waarom het Moses dan beveel…?” Matt 19:7). Jesus het hulle reggehelp: Moses het dit as regulerende gevalsreg toegelaat weens die hardheid van menseharte (sklerokardia). Dit keur nie egskeiding goed nie, maar beperk misbruik.

Deuteronomic Case Law Flowchart · Deut 24:1–4 Conditional Structure 申命记 24:1–4 条件判例法流程图 Deuteronomiese Gevalsreg-Vloeidiagram · Deut 24:1–4 Voorwaardelike Struktuur
01 · PROTASIS (IF — vv. 1–3)

Condition: If a man marries a wife, finds 'erwat dabar in her, writes a certificate of divorce, sends her away, and she marries a second man who later divorces her or dies…

假设条件（若）：如果人娶妻之后，见她有 'erwat dabar，就写休书交在她手中打发她离开，而她再嫁后又被后夫休弃或后夫死了……

Voorwaarde (As): As ’n man ’n vrou neem, 'erwat dabar by haar vind, ’n skeibrief skryf, haar wegstuur, en sy met ’n tweede man trou wat later van haar skei of sterf…

02 · APODOSIS (THEN — v. 4)

The Only Actual Command: Then the first husband is strictly forbidden from taking her back again—stopping casual spouse-trading and hasty impulse divorces.

唯一真正的禁令（则）：那么前夫不可再娶她为妻——藉此遏止草率休妻与变相换妻的恶行。

Die Enigste Werklike Gebod (Dan): Dan mag die eerste man haar beslis nie weer terugneem nie—om ligtelike egskeiding en uitbuiting te keer.

03 · DUAL MOSAIC PURPOSE

1) Concession to Hardness of Heart (sklerokardia): Civil law accommodated fallen reality without endorsing it (Calvin).
2) Protection for the Wife: The certificate proved she was legally dismissed, protecting her from future claims by the first husband and clearing her from false charges of adultery.

1) 对人心刚硬的容忍（sklerokardia）：加尔文指出，民事容许并不等同于神眼中的道德许可。
2) 对弱势妻子的保护：休书证明她已合法离异，免受前夫日后的纠缠与勒索，也保护她免遭奸淫的诬告。

1) Toegewing aan Hardheid van Hart (sklerokardia): Burgerlike wet het sonde beperk sonder om dit goed te keur (Calvyn).
2) Beskerming vir die Vrou: Die skeibrief het haar vrygespreek van valse owerspelaanklagte en verdere aansprake deur die eerste man.

Point 03 · Genesis 1:27 & 2:24 Design 第三点 · 创世记 1:27 与 2:24 的创造蓝图 Punt 03 · Genesis 1:27 & 2:24 Skeppingsontwerp

What God Originally Purposed 神起初创造的心意 Wat God Oorspronklik Beoog Het

Notice Jesus’ hermeneutic: He superseded the Pharisees’ appeal to Mosaic case law by returning to God’s original creation design. Love, sex, and marriage belong to God’s image-bearing purpose for humanity—male and female united in lifelong covenant.

请注意耶稣的解经方法：祂越过法利赛人所引用的摩西民事妥协条例，直接回到神起初创造的设计。爱、性与婚姻是神按着自己形象造男造女、建立终身盟约的原有旨意。

Let op Jesus se uitlegmetode: Hy het die Fariseërs se beroep op Mosaïese gevalsreg oortref deur terug te keer na God se oorspronklike skeppingsontwerp. Liefde, seksualiteit en die huwelik behoort tot God se beelddraende doel vir die mensdom—man en vrou verenig in ’n lewenslange verbond.

Point 04 · Authority, Exceptions & Pastoral Boundaries 第四点 · 主耶稣的权威教导与牧养边界 Punt 04 · Gesag, Uitsonderings & Pastorale Grense

What Jesus Said (Matt 5:32; 19:9; Luke 16:18) 主耶稣说什么（太 5:32；19:9；路 16:18） Wat Jesus Gesê Het (Matt 5:32; 19:9; Luk 16:18)

With the authoritative formula “But I tell you” (ego de lego hymin—the same authority used against anger in v. 22 and lust in v. 28), Jesus dismantles “any-cause” divorce and teaches two core truths:

耶稣再次使用极具权威的句式“只是我告诉你们”（ego de lego hymin——正如在 22 节论发怒、28 节论情欲时一样），彻底推翻“任何理由皆可离婚”的文化，确立两项核心真理：

Met die gesaghebbende formule “Maar Ek sê vir julle” (ego de lego hymin—dieselfde gesag as teen woede in v. 22 en wellus in v. 28), verwerp Jesus “enige-rede”-egskeiding en leer twee kernwaarhede:

01 · Legitimate Ground (Permitted ≠ Commanded)01 · 合法例外（容许 ≠ 命令）01 · Geldige Grond (Toegelaat ≠ Beveel)

Jesus recognizes porneia (persistent, unrepentant sexual immorality) as a covenant-rupturing ground where divorce is permitted—yet never commanded. Because God calls us to forgive, repentance and reconciliation must always be pursued first. 耶稣承认 porneia（持久、不悔改的性淫乱）从根本上破坏了婚姻盟约，因此离婚是被允许的，但绝非被命令的。神呼召我们彼此饶恕，因此应当首先寻求悔改与和好。 Jesus erken porneia (volhardende seksuele ontrou) as ’n verbondbrekende grond waar egskeiding toegelaat word—dog nooit beveel nie. Vergifnis en versoening moet altyd eerste nagestreef word.

02 · Rejecting “Any-Cause” Divorce02 · 拒绝随意离婚02 · Verwerping van “Enige Rede”-Egskeiding

Divorcing without biblical grounds causes adultery (moicheuthenai). A human divorce certificate cannot dissolve what God has joined together; it pushes the dismissed spouse—and anyone who marries her—into an adulterous union. 没有合乎圣经依据的离婚，会使人陷在奸淫里（moicheuthenai）。一纸属人的休书不能废除神所配合的联合，反倒会把被休者及迎娶她的人推入奸淫的关系中。 Egskeiding sonder Bybelse gronde veroorsaak owerspel (moicheuthenai). ’n Menslike skeibrief kan nie ontbind wat God saamgevoeg het nie; dit dryf die weggestuurde maat—en wie haar trou—in owerspel in.

Biblical Synthesis: Abandonment, Abuse & Gospel Grace 全本圣经的整全视角：遗弃、家暴与福音的恩典 Bybelse Sintese: Verlating, Mishandeling & Evangelie-Genade

Apostolic Witness · 1 Corinthians 7:10–11, 15 使徒的见证 · 哥林多前书 7:10–11, 15 Apostoliese Getuienis · 1 Korintiërs 7:10–11, 15

10“A wife must not separate from her husband. 11But if she does, she must remain unmarried or else be reconciled to her husband. And a husband must not divorce his wife… 15But if the unbeliever leaves, let it be so. The brother or the sister is not bound in such circumstances; God has called us to live in peace.”

10「妻子不可离开丈夫，11若是离开了，不可再嫁，或是仍同丈夫和好。丈夫也不可离弃妻子。……15倘若那不信的人要离去，就由他离去吧！无论是弟兄，是姐妹，遇着这样的事都不必拘束。神召我们原是要我们和睦。」

10“’n Vrou moet nie van haar man skei nie; 11maar as sy tog skei, moet sy ongetroud bly of haar met haar man versoen; en ’n man moet sy vrou nie verlaat nie… 15Maar as die ongelowige wil skei, laat hom skei; die broer of die suster is in sulke gevalle nie gebonde nie, maar God het ons tot vrede geroep.”

1. Desertion by an Unbeliever (1 Cor 7:15): Paul applies Jesus’ teaching—believers stay married (vv. 10–11), yet if an unbelieving spouse obstinately abandons the covenant, the deserted believer is “not bound.”
2. Physical Abuse & Criminal Harm: As D. A. Carson emphasizes on Matthew 19, the Bible never sanctions ignoring physical abuse; violent abuse is a criminal and immoral matter requiring civil court protection and prosecution to keep victims safe.
3. Grace for the Divorced & Remarried: Even when past failures occurred outside biblical exceptions, repentant divorced and remarried Christians belong fully to the body of Christ and are called to honor God in their present lives and marriages.

1. 不信配偶的遗弃（林前 7:15）：保罗延展主的教导——信徒应当留守婚姻（10–11节）；但若不信的一方执意离弃盟约，被遗弃的信徒便“不必拘束”。
2. 肢体虐待与犯罪伤害：正如卡森（D. A. Carson）在论及马太福音 19 章时所强调，圣经绝不允许姑息暴力虐待；家暴是刑事犯罪与不义之事，应当寻求法律保护与司法追究以保障受害者安全。
3. 给离婚与再婚者的福音恩典：即使过去曾因不合圣经的缘由离婚或再婚，悔改信靠基督的肢体仍然完全属于基督的身体，并蒙召在当下的生命与婚姻中荣耀神。

1. Verlating deur ’n Ongelowige (1 Kor 7:15): Paulus pas Jesus se leer toe—gelowiges bly getroud (vv. 10–11), maar as ’n ongelowige maat hardnekkig die verbond verbreek en vertrek, is die gelowige “nie gebonde nie.”
2. Fisiese Mishandeling & Kriminele Skade: Soos D. A. Carson op Matteus 19 beklemtoon, keur die Bybel nooit die ignorering van fisiese mishandeling goed nie; geweld vereis regsbeskerming en vervolging om slagoffers te beskerm.
3. Genade vir Geskeides & Hertroudes: Selfs waar vorige mislukkings voorgekom het, behoort berouvolle geskeide en hertroude Christene ten volle tot die liggaam van Christus.

实证研究打破了“基督徒的离婚率与世俗社会无异”的流言。真正决定婚姻稳固性的，不是挂名的宗教标签，而是积极参与的门徒操练——在控制社会经济变量的研究中，活跃信仰群体离婚风险降低 31% 至 97%。
